Kita, an AI credit assessment startup in Y Combinator's Winter 2026 batch, announced in August that it raised **$4.5 million in seed funding** led by BoxGroup.

The round included Y Combinator, Golden Gate Ventures, U.S. News Digital Ventures, BEENEXT, Kaya Founders, Genting Ventures and Apex Star Capital, [according to the company](https://www.kita.ai/blog/kita-seed-round).

Kita builds software for lenders that evaluate borrowers using financial documents such as bank statements, payslips and invoices. [Y Combinator describes the company](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/kita) as automating parts of application completion, document verification and underwriting by turning unstructured borrower documents into data that credit teams can use.

The company was founded in 2025 by **Carmel Limcaoco and Rhea Malhotra**. Limcaoco, Kita's CEO, is from Manila, while Malhotra is its CTO. Y Combinator says both were completing master's degrees in computer science at Stanford when they chose to build Kita full time.

Kita says its platform is live with lenders in the **Philippines, Indonesia, Mexico and the United States**. The company said it plans to use the funding to expand its engineering team, develop its underwriting and fraud capabilities, and support growth across multiple markets.
