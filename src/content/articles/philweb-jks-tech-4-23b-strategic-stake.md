PhilWeb Corp. and its wholly owned subsidiary PhilWeb Capital Corp. have agreed to invest a combined **₱4.226 billion** in JKS Tech Solutions Inc., a Philippine technology and digital infrastructure company serving licensed operators in the digital entertainment sector. ([PSE EDGE](https://edge.pse.com.ph/downloadHtml.do?file_id=1965551))

Under the agreements, PhilWeb will subscribe to **3.41 million JKS Common B shares** at ₱394 each for about **₱1.34 billion**. PhilWeb Capital will subscribe to another **7.32 million shares** at the same price for roughly **₱2.88 billion**. Together, the subscriptions are intended to give the PhilWeb group an approximately **30% strategic equity interest in JKS**. ([PSE EDGE](https://edge.pse.com.ph/downloadHtml.do?file_id=1965551))

JKS is separately buying **81.38 million PhilWeb treasury shares** at ₱16.50 each for about **₱1.34 billion**. Those shares represent approximately **4.85% of PhilWeb's issued and outstanding shares**. PhilWeb said proceeds from the treasury-share sale will partly fund its own subscription in JKS. ([PSE EDGE](https://edge.pse.com.ph/downloadHtml.do?file_id=1965337))

PhilWeb describes JKS as a business-to-business technology, platform and digital infrastructure company serving licensed mid-market operators in the Philippine digital entertainment sector. PAGCOR lists JKS as an accredited gaming system administrator for the Epic Game brand. ([PSE EDGE](https://edge.pse.com.ph/downloadHtml.do?file_id=1965551))

The Philippine Stock Exchange classified the transaction under its rules governing substantial acquisitions and reverse takeovers and suspended trading in PhilWeb shares beginning September 8 while awaiting the required comprehensive disclosure. The classification does **not itself mean the deal is a reverse takeover**. ([PSE EDGE](https://edge.pse.com.ph/downloadHtml.do?file_id=1965527))

The subscriptions remain subject to JKS shareholder approval, Securities and Exchange Commission approval, other regulatory requirements and the terms of the agreements. ([PSE EDGE](https://edge.pse.com.ph/downloadHtml.do?file_id=1965551))
