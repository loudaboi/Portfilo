Microfinance lender **OnePuhunan** has secured more than **₱1.5 billion in financing from Citi and German development finance institution DEG** to expand lending to underserved entrepreneurs in the Philippines. ([The Philippine Star](https://www.philstar.com/business/2026/09/09/2554891/citi-deg-extend-over-p15-billion-onepuhunan))

Citi is providing more than **₱300 million directly** and coordinated the financing arrangement, mobilizing more than **₱1.2 billion from DEG**, which is part of Germany's KfW Group. ([The Philippine Star](https://www.philstar.com/business/2026/09/09/2554891/citi-deg-extend-over-p15-billion-onepuhunan))

The capital will support OnePuhunan's microfinance portfolio, particularly financing for **Filipino women running micro-businesses**. OnePuhunan said the funding will also support the expansion of its reach and the digitalization of its operations and financial products. ([The Philippine Star](https://www.philstar.com/business/2026/09/09/2554891/citi-deg-extend-over-p15-billion-onepuhunan))

OnePuhunan is the operating name of **CreditAccess Philippines Financing Company Inc.**, an SEC-regulated financing company focused on lower-income individuals and small businesses that are underserved by traditional banks. The company says it has operated in the Philippines since 2014. ([OnePuhunan](https://www.onepuhunan.com.ph/))

As of 2025, OnePuhunan had a gross loan portfolio of more than **₱10 billion** and served more than **600,000 borrowers through 315 branches across 16 regions**, according to reporting on the transaction. ([The Philippine Star](https://www.philstar.com/business/2026/09/09/2554891/citi-deg-extend-over-p15-billion-onepuhunan))

The transaction is also **DEG's first debt investment in the Philippine financial inclusion sector** and Citi's first Social Finance partnership with DEG in the country. ([InsiderPH](https://insiderph.com/citi-deg-seal-p15-b-loan-to-boost-women-entrepreneurs))
