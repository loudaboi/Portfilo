The Philippine government has launched the finalized **Philippines AI+ Infrastructure Masterplan 2026–2033**, a roadmap estimating that **$34.4 billion in public and private investment** will be required to expand the country's infrastructure for artificial intelligence. ([Philippine News Agency](https://www.pna.gov.ph/articles/1283577))

The plan targets a roughly **30-fold increase in AI data center capacity**, from about 50 megawatts today to **1.5 gigawatts by 2033**. Its first phase calls for roughly 400 MW of capacity by 2030. ([Philippine News Agency](https://www.pna.gov.ph/articles/1283577))

Of the estimated investment required, the masterplan expects **$13.5 billion**, or 39%, to come from public sources and around **$21 billion**, or 61%, from private investors. These are funding requirements and assumptions in the roadmap, not money that has already been committed. ([Philippine News Agency](https://www.pna.gov.ph/articles/1283577))

The infrastructure plan identifies **Clark-Bataan** as its primary anchor, with Batangas-Aurora, Subic and Calabarzon serving as additional corridors or hubs. Cebu, Iloilo, Davao and Cagayan de Oro are identified as future regional nodes. ([Philippine News Agency](https://www.pna.gov.ph/articles/1283577))

The roadmap also calls for the reskilling of **1.3 million IT-BPM workers for AI-enabled services by 2033**. It projects more than 500,000 AI-related jobs and another 175,000 infrastructure jobs, although those figures remain government projections rather than measured outcomes. ([Philippine News Agency](https://www.pna.gov.ph/articles/1283577))
