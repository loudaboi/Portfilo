Qualcomm and Amazon have announced a multigeneration collaboration to develop **customized silicon for large-scale AI data centers**, with the companies focusing on chips used for AI inference and high-speed networking inside Amazon Web Services infrastructure. ([Qualcomm](https://investor.qualcomm.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2026/Qualcomm-Announces-Multi-Generational-Product-Collaboration-with-Amazon-to-Build-Next-Generation-AI-Data-Center-Infrastructure/default.aspx))

The partnership also covers **optical connectivity technology reaching up to 1.6 terabits per second**, with Qualcomm supplying technologies including SerDes and optical digital signal processors for links inside data center networks. ([Qualcomm](https://investor.qualcomm.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2026/Qualcomm-Announces-Multi-Generational-Product-Collaboration-with-Amazon-to-Build-Next-Generation-AI-Data-Center-Infrastructure/default.aspx))

Alongside the commercial collaboration, Qualcomm issued an Amazon affiliate a warrant to acquire up to **25 million Qualcomm shares at an exercise price of $161.26 per share**. The warrant expires in September 2036. ([SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/804328/000110465926105718/tm2623289d1_8k.htm))

The shares vest in stages based on commercial agreements, binding purchase orders and actual Amazon purchases of Qualcomm server-chip products, technology, systems and manufacturing services. Qualcomm’s filing ties the full vesting structure to as much as **$60 billion in payments**, but that figure is a maximum threshold under the warrant arrangement rather than $60 billion of purchases already committed. **3.75 million shares vested when the warrant was issued based on initial purchase commitments.** ([SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/804328/000110465926105718/tm2623289d1_8k.htm))

Qualcomm also said it plans to expand its own use of AWS infrastructure, including Amazon Bedrock, for electronic design automation workloads used in chip development. ([Qualcomm](https://investor.qualcomm.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2026/Qualcomm-Announces-Multi-Generational-Product-Collaboration-with-Amazon-to-Build-Next-Generation-AI-Data-Center-Infrastructure/default.aspx))

The agreement adds Amazon to Qualcomm’s broader push into data center computing. In June, Qualcomm said it was targeting **more than $15 billion in annual data center revenue by fiscal 2029**, as the company expands beyond its traditional smartphone-chip business. ([Qualcomm](https://investor.qualcomm.com/news-events/press-releases/news-details/2026/Qualcomm-Accelerates-Diversification-with-Comprehensive-Strategy-for-Data-Center-and-Sees-Multiple-Inflection-Points-Over-the-Next-3-to-5-Years/default.aspx))
