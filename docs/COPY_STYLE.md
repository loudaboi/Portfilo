# Portfilo v1 Copy Style

_Last updated: 2026-09-14_

## Purpose

Portfilo public copy should be clear, concrete, and proportionate to what Portfilo can actually prove or deliver.

AI-generated wording has no automatic authority.

## Core rules

1. Prefer concrete nouns, verbs, systems, outcomes, and constraints over abstract brand language.
2. Do not use agency filler such as "crafting digital experiences", "innovative solutions", or similar interchangeable claims.
3. Do not claim expertise, scale, speed, savings, growth, transformation, or business impact without support.
4. Do not use technical vocabulary merely to make Portfilo sound advanced.
5. Delete redundant copy before rewriting it.
6. Functional labels should be literal.
7. Editorial headlines/deks must remain evidence-bounded.
8. Case-study copy must distinguish what existed, what Portfilo built, and what result is actually evidenced.
9. Newsletter copy must describe the real product. Do not invent cadence or delivery promises.
10. Temporary implementation copy must not silently become final positioning.

## Public positioning gate

Before final homepage positioning, service descriptions, or a major campaign line:

`reference evidence -> approved claims -> message architecture -> founder approval -> wording`

Do not draft a polished answer first and then search for justification.

## Section behavior

### Work

Lead with the business situation, problem, system, and evidenced result.

Technology stack is secondary unless it materially explains the solution.

### Services

Describe business problems and the kinds of systems Portfilo can build to address them.

Avoid a commodity menu of frameworks and platforms.

### Hustle

Treat subjects as people doing real work, not motivational symbols.

### Journal

Write for understanding. Research depth should support the piece without forcing the reader to watch the research process.

### Contact

Keep it literal. Do not add urgency, scarcity, or sales language without a real reason.

## Anti-slop test

Ask:

- Could the line appear unchanged on any agency/SaaS site?
- Is it supported?
- Is it saying something the interface already makes obvious?
- Is it longer because it sounds more professional?
- Would deleting it make the page stronger?

If yes, revise or remove it.
