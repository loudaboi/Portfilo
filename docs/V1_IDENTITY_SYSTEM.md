## Selected direction

**Sharp Ornamental 01**

The founder approved the refined Sharp Ornamental 01 identity study as the current Portfilo identity direction.

## Core identity

Portfilo uses two coordinated but independent assets:

1. a restrained premium wordmark;
2. an independent sharp ornamental emblem.

The emblem does not derive from the wordmark or any Portfilo letterform.

The relationship between the two comes from shared proportion, restraint, contrast, and application.

## Emblem

Approved characteristics:

- four-point ornamental construction;
- narrow, controlled points;
- concave internal curves;
- strong negative-space diamond;
- symmetric primary construction;
- compact silhouette;
- premium monochrome behavior;
- independent recognition without the wordmark.

The emblem should remain simple enough for favicon, social avatar, UI identity, and transition/loading contexts.

### Production status

The approved generated study remains visual reference evidence.

The production emblem has now been redrawn and founder-approved as clean vector geometry with:

- exact geometric symmetry;
- controlled curve tension;
- clean inner/outer apex behavior;
- verified small-size legibility at 16px / 24px / 32px / 48px;
- no raster artifacts;
- no dependency on glow, blur, bevel, gradient, or texture.

## Wordmark

Approved character:

- simple;
- calm;
- premium;
- restrained;
- secondary to the emblem;
- serif-led in the approved study;
- no ligature gimmick;
- no monogram extraction;
- no exaggerated futuristic customization.

Current founder-approved production wordmark:

**PORTFILO / Noto Serif Display SemiCondensed Medium / ALL CAPS**

Production construction:

- `PORTFILO`;
- Noto Serif Display SemiCondensed Medium;
- 0.115em tracking basis;
- vector outlines;
- `currentColor` master;
- fixed ink/off-white exports.

The exact approved geometry originated from:

`research/identity/sharp-ornamental-01/all-caps-a/`

and now replaces the prior title-case wordmark in the canonical final identity set, brand package, and runtime wordmark asset.

The Sharp Ornamental 01 emblem remains unchanged.

## Lockups

Approved applications:

- emblem alone;
- wordmark alone;
- horizontal emblem + wordmark;
- stacked emblem + wordmark.

Spacing and optical alignment must be finalized during production asset development.

## Favicon

Preferred browser favicon:

- emblem only;
- transparent background;
- no enclosing square/circle/tile by default;
- simplified only if necessary for clarity at very small sizes.

Filled dark/light app-icon tiles may exist later for platforms that require a background.

## Color

Primary identity remains monochrome-first:

- black on off-white;
- off-white on black.

Neutral silver is an optional secondary treatment.

The identity must remain strong without silver, gradients, or material effects.

Brand signal color remains open and should not be selected merely to decorate the identity.

## Responsive Identity relationship

Responsive Identity remains the wider creative system.

The logo itself does not need to constantly morph.

Responsive behavior should primarily come from:

- scale;
- placement;
- transition;
- composition;
- surrounding typography;
- media relationships;
- surface state;
- controlled motion.

The emblem may participate in motion later, but static recognition is primary.

## Rejected identity routes

Do not reopen without a new founder decision:

- former italic Portfilo wordmark as default;
- old favicon/symbol;
- fi ligature logo;
- P/PF monograms;
- symbol extracted from the wordmark;
- star-cluster / Omen-like composition;
- generic AI/crypto/software marks;
- ornamental marks that require effects to look premium.
