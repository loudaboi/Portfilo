# Portfilo v1 Photography Treatment

_Last updated: 2026-09-14_

## Source integrity

A supplied photograph is the source of truth.

Permitted production changes may include:

- crop;
- exposure/contrast correction;
- white balance;
- restrained sharpening;
- noise/grain treatment;
- resize/export optimization.

Do not add, remove, relocate, or reconstruct people, products, logos, documents, environments, or events unless the result is clearly an illustration rather than documentary material.

## Color

Natural color is allowed and often preferred for Hustle and real-world project context.

Do not force photography into monochrome merely because Portfilo's interface is neutral.

A monochrome treatment may be used when a specific composition is approved and it does not reduce documentary clarity.

## AI editing

Do not use generative editing to create factual-looking documentary content.

If generative editing materially changes what appears to have existed in the photographed scene, the result is not documentary evidence and must not be presented as such.
