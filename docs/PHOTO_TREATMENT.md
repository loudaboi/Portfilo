# Portfilo Photography Treatment

_Last updated: 2026-09-04_

_Status: APPROVED / CANONICAL FOR USER-SUPPLIED PHOTOGRAPHY_

## Purpose

Define one consistent production treatment for user-supplied Portfilo photography without altering the depicted scene.

Approved treatment name:

`PORTFILO EDITORIAL MONOCHROME v1`

Approved by:

`Louda Tuppal`

Approval date:

`2026-09-04`

## Source-image authority

The supplied photograph is the visual source of truth.

Post-processing may improve tonal consistency, grain, sharpness, crop, resolution and delivery quality, but it must not reinterpret or reconstruct the scene.

Never alter:

- people or characters;
- faces;
- bodies or anatomy;
- poses or gestures;
- clothing;
- objects;
- furniture;
- architecture;
- drawings or documents;
- spatial relationships;
- natural blur, focus or shadows in a way that changes what is depicted.

Do not add or remove people, objects, text, environmental details or narrative elements.

Do not use generative fill, generative replacement or generative scene reconstruction as part of this treatment.

## Canonical treatment

Apply the following consistently unless Louda explicitly approves an exception:

- black-and-white / grayscale only;
- slightly dark editorial exposure;
- deep blacks with preserved useful shadow detail;
- controlled highlights without aggressive clipping;
- moderate readable contrast rather than HDR-style contrast;
- medium-visible monochrome grain;
- print-like / film-like grain character rather than digital-noise appearance;
- grain must remain perceptible at intended display size;
- mild sharpening only;
- no artificial HDR, haloing, excessive clarity or plastic smoothing;
- cleanup only when it does not change real image content;
- preserve natural blur, focus, shadows and source-image imperfections;
- no Portfilo blue/red baked into photography by default;
- brand color remains in the surrounding UI unless a later approved treatment explicitly changes this.

## Crop and geometry

Cropping is allowed when required by a real layout.

Do not:

- stretch the image;
- warp people or objects;
- alter perspective to manufacture a different scene;
- distort geometry to make a crop fit.

When cropping, preserve the source image's important subject relationships and visual logic.

## Resolution and export

Upscaling is allowed only for delivery quality.

Upscaling must not intentionally invent or reconstruct new scene content.

Use a consistent export pipeline across a set so perceived contrast, grain and sharpness remain coherent from image to image.

Final file format, dimensions and compression may vary by surface, but the visual treatment must remain consistent.

## First approved reference

The first approved treatment test used the user-supplied black-and-white workspace photograph `IMG_4586.jpeg`.

The processed result was approved by Louda Tuppal on 2026-09-04 and establishes the baseline visual treatment for subsequent supplied photographs.

The approved treatment changed image quality and tone only. It did not authorize any alteration of the depicted person, workspace, objects or composition.

## Relationship to generated imagery

This workflow is separate from image generation.

User-supplied photography should not be routed through the generated-image concept workflow merely because post-processing is needed.

For supplied photography, the source photograph has scene authority and Portfilo applies only the controlled production treatment defined here unless a separate editorial or rights decision requires otherwise.
