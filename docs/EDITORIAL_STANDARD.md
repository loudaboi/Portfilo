# Portfilo Editorial Standard

## Mission

Portfilo should help ambitious Filipinos make better decisions about work, money, skills, building, and life through useful, specific, evidence-aware journalism and analysis.

Portfilo is not a motivation feed, content farm, or generic self-improvement publication.

## Core editorial principles

### 1. Accuracy before fluency

A sentence that sounds convincing but cannot be supported does not belong in a factual story.

Do not publish a factual claim because it is plausible, common knowledge on social media, or generated confidently by AI.

### 2. Research before prose

The reporting question and evidence come before the article.

The workflow is not:

`topic -> AI -> article`

It is:

`question -> research -> evidence -> story decision -> draft -> verification -> publication`

### 3. Useful specificity

Prefer concrete Philippine context over generic advice.

Weak:
> Negotiate your salary.

Better:
> What compensation evidence can a Filipino applicant use when negotiating a role, and when does negotiation actually change the outcome?

### 4. Distinguish kinds of claims

Portfilo should keep these conceptually separate:

- **Fact** - directly supported by credible evidence.
- **Reported claim** - attributed to a person or organization but not independently established as underlying fact.
- **Estimate** - calculated from stated inputs/assumptions.
- **Inference** - Portfilo's interpretation of evidence.
- **Opinion / recommendation** - Portfilo's judgment.

Readers should not have to guess which category a consequential statement belongs to.

### 5. Preserve uncertainty

If the evidence cannot establish something, say so.

Acceptable:
> We could not establish her personal income from public records.

Unacceptable:
> Her estimated net worth is probably...

Do not convert missing evidence into fake precision.

### 6. Headlines must not outrun evidence

A headline may be sharper than the body, but never more certain than the reporting.

Avoid clickbait that changes:

- "some" into "all";
- correlation into causation;
- an advertised salary into actual earnings;
- anecdotes into population-level facts;
- an estimate into a measured number.

### 7. Local context is part of the value

When relevant, investigate how global ideas interact with Philippine realities such as:

- wages and purchasing power;
- taxes and mandatory contributions;
- family obligations;
- geography and commuting;
- local labor markets;
- remote/global work;
- education pathways;
- infrastructure;
- regulation;
- culture and professional networks.

Do not force a Philippine angle when it adds no value.

### 8. Original contribution

A substantial Portfilo story should contain at least one meaningful contribution such as:

- original interview;
- primary-source research;
- original calculation;
- original dataset or structured sample;
- useful synthesis of difficult information;
- Philippine-specific application;
- case study;
- a conclusion that genuinely emerged from research.

Rewriting existing articles is not enough.

## Content types

Use explicit editorial types.

### Reported

Original reporting, interviews, documents, data, or direct observation.

### Explained

Clarifies a complex system or question using evidence.

### Guide

Research-backed practical instructions or decision support.

### Profile

A reported examination of a person, company, group, or path. A profile is not a rewritten biography or press release.

### Analysis

Portfilo's interpretation of established evidence.

### Perspective

Clearly identified argument or opinion.

### Field Notes

Observations from events, workplaces, communities, interviews, or field reporting. Separate observation from generalization.

### Partner

Commercial content. Must be unmistakably labeled and subject to separate commercial rules.

## AI use

AI can assist with:

- query generation;
- source discovery leads;
- document organization;
- summarization;
- extracting candidate claims;
- comparing sources;
- identifying contradictions;
- calculation/code assistance;
- interview-question development;
- structure;
- copy editing;
- adversarial review.

AI must not be treated as:

- a source;
- evidence;
- verification;
- a substitute for reporting;
- authority for a quotation, statistic, attribution, law, or current fact.

Any factual material surfaced by AI must be independently checked before publication.

## Profiles and interviews

Separate:

1. what the subject says;
2. what Portfilo can independently verify;
3. what other credible sources say;
4. Portfilo's interpretation.

Do not turn self-description into independent fact.

Quotes must come from a real source available to the reporting process. Never reconstruct or improve a quote while presenting it as verbatim.

## Data and numbers

For consequential numbers, ask:

- who measured it?
- what period?
- what population?
- what sample?
- average or median?
- nominal or adjusted?
- administrative data or survey?
- advertised amount or realized amount?
- what limitations matter?
- does another credible dataset conflict?

Do not hide limitations that materially affect interpretation.

## Corrections

Portfilo corrects factual errors transparently.

Material corrections should remain attached to the story with:

- correction date;
- what was wrong;
- what is correct.

Do not silently rewrite a consequential error in a way that conceals the correction.

Minor typographical or formatting fixes do not require a formal correction unless they change meaning.

## Commercial independence

Sponsors, advertisers, partners, or affiliates may not determine the factual conclusion of editorial coverage.

Commercial material must be clearly labeled.

Do not disguise advertising as independent reporting.

If a commercial relationship is relevant to the reader's interpretation of a story, disclose it.

## Tone

Portfilo should be:

- precise;
- curious;
- skeptical without being cynical;
- readable without being simplistic;
- ambitious without hustle-culture theatrics;
- confident when evidence is strong;
- explicit when evidence is weak.

Avoid:

- fake profundity;
- generic motivational language;
- inflated certainty;
- corporate jargon;
- formulaic AI openings;
- unnecessary listicles;
- repetitive summaries;
- filler conclusions.

## Publication test

Before publication, ask:

1. What did we actually find?
2. What evidence supports the central claim?
3. What would a skeptical reader challenge?
4. Did we preserve the most important caveat?
5. Is the headline fair?
6. Are facts, estimates, inference, and opinion distinguishable?
7. Does the story materially help the target reader?
8. Would we be comfortable showing the source trail?
