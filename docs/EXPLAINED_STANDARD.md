# Portfilo Explained Directive

_Last updated: 2026-09-07_

_Status: FOUNDER DIRECTIVE PRESERVED / STRUCTURAL IMPLEMENTATION NOT YET CANONICAL_

## Why this file was corrected

An earlier version of this file was promoted too quickly into a canonical `Explained` standard after the `starting-a-business` drafts failed.

The founder/editor had approved the **editorial principle** behind the correction, but the file also contained structural and writing rules that had not yet been independently grounded in professional form research.

That was a process error under `docs/STYLE_RESEARCH_STANDARD.md`.

This file now preserves only the founder-approved direction. A specific Explained structure must still come from story-specific professional form research, relevant human-edited exemplars, and an approved style brief.

## Founder-approved direction

Portfilo `Explained` exists to help the audience understand a subject.

It is not a literature review and should not make the reader watch the newsroom perform its research process.

> **Research everything needed for confidence. Show only what the reader needs for understanding.**

Research remains the evidentiary foundation. It does not automatically determine how many sources, studies, experts, statistics, sections, or caveats must appear in the public article.

## What this directive does not authorize

This file does **not** authorize AI to decide:

- the number of sections;
- a `three-part`, `five-part`, or other symmetrical architecture;
- a fixed question sequence;
- a named framework;
- a particular lead type;
- a particular ending shape;
- how many sources should be visible;
- a universal Portfilo explainer template.

Those decisions must be justified by:

1. the actual reader need;
2. the evidence shape;
3. documented professional practice appropriate to the assignment;
4. relevant human-edited exemplars;
5. explicit human editorial approval.

## Evidence visibility

The founder/editor has explicitly rejected drafts that displayed research volume instead of explaining the subject.

Sources should appear in the public article because they perform a necessary reader-facing job, not because Portfilo wants to prove that research happened.

The research packet remains available to support claim verification, contradiction search, audit, and later editing even when much of that work stays backstage.

## AI boundary

Simplifying AI-written prose is not enough if AI also invented the article shape.

A draft can still be AI slop even when:

- the language is plain;
- citations are reduced;
- the facts are correct;
- the sections sound reasonable.

If the structural choices do not have authority outside the model's own intuition, the form has not passed Portfilo's writing system.

## Current starting-a-business correction

`research/stories/starting-a-business/style-research-v2.md` re-researches the Explainer form from documented professional guidance and human-edited examples after Draft 3 failed.

That record currently recommends a specific method for this story, but the method is **not approved merely because the research file recommends it**.

The founder/editor must approve the structural method before `style-brief.md` is revised and before another draft begins.

## Existing stories

Existing published Portfilo stories are not protected as writing precedent merely because they were published earlier.

They may be separately audited against the current research, writing, and style-research standards. Any decision to retain, revise, unpublish, or remove an existing story requires its own editorial review and approval.
