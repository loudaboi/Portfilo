## Shared constraints

All concepts must:

- use Sharp Ornamental 01 + the approved Portfilo wordmark;
- preserve Problem-led software positioning;
- preserve Work / Services / Hustle / Journal / Contact architecture;
- avoid fake Work, fake screenshots, fake testimonials, fake product UI, fake metrics;
- allow different Portfilo surfaces to have different atmospheres;
- make motion perform a state/navigation/content job rather than decorative fade-up;
- remain accessible and coherent with reduced motion;
- avoid card soup, bento grids, generic dark SaaS, glass, neon AI gradients, terminal cosplay, chrome blobs, and giant static hero type as the entire idea.

# Concept 01 — Aperture

## Thesis

**Portfilo is revealed through controlled openings.**

Aperture uses the Sharp Ornamental emblem as the underlying spatial logic without turning the logo itself into a gimmick.

The homepage begins mostly off-white with a tightly composed header and a large black field occupying part of the viewport. That field behaves like an aperture: it opens, contracts, and reframes content as the user moves through the site.

The emblem is used sparingly as a framing/masking cue, not as giant decoration.

## Atmosphere

- light-first;
- off-white paper-like field;
- true black;
- restrained neutral silver;
- contextual media color only when real content exists.

This is the most fashion/editorial of the three, but the motion and structure keep it digital.

## Hero

Composition:

- small wordmark top-left;
- nav top-right;
- positioning copy sits lower-left, not oversized across the full screen;
- a large geometric black aperture occupies approximately 40–55% of the viewport;
- emblem appears as a small precision mark, not a hero graphic.

The opening line remains:

**Software built around the problem.**

It is treated as a compact editorial statement, not a giant billboard.

## Signature interaction

As scroll begins, the black aperture changes proportion and reveals the next content state.

The user is not merely scrolling down sections.

The same spatial opening reframes:

- services;
- future Work media;
- Hustle photography;
- Journal titles.

The aperture is the consistent spatial behavior.

## Services

Services appear as a typographic sequence around the aperture.

Selecting or hovering a problem territory changes the aperture size/position and reveals the relevant capability pair.

No cards.

## Work

When real Work exists, project media occupies the aperture itself.

The aperture becomes a true media stage rather than a decorative shape.

Until real Work exists, no project media is fabricated.

## Journal

The aperture collapses away.

The page becomes almost entirely off-white and editorial.

Journal rows feel like a reading index, with minimal motion.

## Hustle

Authentic photography can break the rigid aperture proportions and become the most human scene on the page.

## Mobile

The aperture becomes a simpler vertical framing device rather than a complex scroll-linked composition.

## Strength

Strongest balance of:

- premium;
- editorial;
- identity-led;
- contemporary;
- usable before Work exists.

## Risk

If the aperture geometry is too literal or repeated too often, it can become a visual gimmick.

# Concept 02 — Relay

## Thesis

**The page behaves like a sequence of states passing control from one surface to the next.**

Relay is darker, more technical, and more software-native than Aperture.

It does not use a conventional stack of sections.

A fixed identity/control rail remains present while the primary viewport changes state.

## Atmosphere

- graphite / near-black base;
- off-white type;
- neutral silver;
- occasional off-white full-viewport inversions;
- contextual content color.

The difference from the rejected homepage is that darkness is not the design idea. State change is.

## Hero

The first viewport is deliberately sparse.

Instead of giant type:

- emblem sits at a strong but compact scale;
- wordmark and navigation form a control rail;
- positioning copy occupies one controlled block;
- a large active-state field shows the current Portfilo mode: `STUDIO`.

The interface feels almost like a high-end digital instrument, but without fake controls or terminal language.

## Signature interaction

Scroll or navigation passes a visible state from:

`STUDIO -> WORK -> SERVICES -> HUSTLE -> JOURNAL`

Each state changes:

- background;
- content alignment;
- scale;
- active typography;
- media behavior.

A slim state rail shows where the user is.

The handoff between states is the memorable behavior.

## Services

Services become the most interactive state.

Problem territories act as the input.

Capabilities respond as the output.

The interaction can be immediate, precise, and almost tool-like.

## Work

Work becomes the most spatial state once real case studies exist.

A selected project can occupy most of the viewport, while evidence/metadata remain on the control rail.

## Journal

The system deliberately deactivates.

Dark switches to off-white.

Motion becomes minimal.

The control rail remains as a subtle identity anchor.

## Hustle

Photography dominates and can temporarily suppress the technical rail.

## Mobile

State rail becomes a compact top/bottom indicator.

No pinned desktop choreography.

## Strength

Strongest software-studio fit and strongest motion-system potential.

## Risk

Highest risk of feeling like a developer/product site if the control language becomes too literal.

# Concept 03 — Cut Scene

## Thesis

**Portfilo changes atmosphere dramatically, but every scene is cut by the same visual logic.**

Cut Scene is the most cinematic and art-directed direction.

The page is not one continuous visual environment.

Each major surface behaves almost like a separate campaign scene.

The identity system connects them through precise cuts, transitions, scale, and emblem placement.

## Atmosphere

Deliberate contrast:

- off-white hero;
- black Services scene;
- contextual-color Work scene;
- photographic Hustle scene;
- quiet off-white Journal scene;
- dark closing Newsletter/Contact state.

The site feels authored through contrast rather than visual uniformity.

## Hero

A composed editorial opening:

- oversized emblem used partially off-canvas;
- small wordmark;
- compact positioning statement;
- asymmetrical layout;
- no conventional centered hero.

The emblem is not decorative wallpaper. Its crop defines the composition.

## Signature interaction

Transitions between sections are scene cuts.

A line, crop, or emblem edge travels across the viewport and reveals a completely different atmosphere.

Transitions should be fast and controlled, closer to editorial/video cutting than smooth floating animation.

## Services

A near-black scene with large typographic problem territories.

Selection snaps the composition into a new arrangement rather than merely changing hover color.

## Work

Each project may establish its own visual world.

The site does not force client/project media into Portfilo monochrome.

## Hustle

This is the strongest photography-led scene.

Portraits can be full-bleed.

## Journal

A complete reduction in intensity.

Quiet typography, off-white background, restrained index.

## Mobile

Atmosphere shifts remain, but scene transitions become simple cuts/crossfades.

## Strength

Highest long-term visual ceiling once Portfilo has strong Work and Hustle media.

## Risk

Weakest current-state fit because Portfilo does not yet have enough real Work/Hustle media to carry the most expressive scenes.

## Recommendation

**Concept 01 — Aperture**

Reason:

- it works with the current lack of Work case studies;
- it uses the approved emblem as spatial inspiration without turning the logo into decoration;
- it gives Portfilo a premium light-first environment rather than returning to generic dark-tech;
- it leaves space for future Work/Hustle media to become increasingly important;
- it supports strong motion without requiring WebGL;
- it lets Journal remain editorial and Services remain interactive;
- it feels most compatible with the new premium wordmark and Sharp Ornamental emblem.
