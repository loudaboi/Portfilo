## Objective

Develop a simple, premium Portfilo identity consisting of:

1. a restrained wordmark;
2. an independent abstract emblem.

The two assets should feel compatible but do not need to share literal geometry or derive from one another.

The identity should feel contemporary, premium, stylistic, and confident without becoming fashion cosplay or generic software branding.

## Founder reference

The founder approved the **identity attitude** seen in the supplied Omen Studios / Vivet-style reference:

- independent emblem;
- quiet wordmark;
- monochrome confidence;
- compact symbol;
- strong negative space;
- fashion-adjacent restraint;
- premium presentation without visual noise.

The reference is **not** a form template.

Do not copy:

- the four-point star/petal forms;
- the rotational composition;
- the exact silver-on-black treatment;
- the emblem silhouette;
- the centered campaign composition;
- any distinctive geometry from the reference.

Use it only as a taste and restraint benchmark.

## Core identity model

### Wordmark

The wordmark should be:

- simple;
- restrained;
- premium;
- highly legible;
- primarily static;
- strong at small header scale;
- distinctive through proportion, spacing, optical refinement, and subtle custom lettering;
- not dependent on a gimmick.

Do not use:

- forced ligatures;
- obvious monograms;
- exaggerated futuristic cuts;
- stencil-tech styling;
- racing/sports slashes;
- decorative flourishes added only to appear luxurious.

### Symbol

The symbol should be:

- independent from the wordmark;
- abstract;
- compact;
- memorable;
- stylistically premium;
- strong in one color;
- recognizable at 16–32px;
- usable as favicon, social avatar, compact UI identity, and transition/loading state;
- visually interesting before any meaning is explained.

The symbol does not need literal semantic meaning.

It should not attempt to illustrate "software," "portfolio," "AI," "technology," or "business."

## Exploration territories

### Territory 01 — Soft Geometric

**Priority: 70%**

This is the recommended primary direction.

Characteristics:

- abstract;
- sculpted;
- controlled curvature;
- soft/sharp tension;
- strong negative space;
- compact silhouette;
- calm rather than aggressive;
- symmetrical or near-symmetrical only when it improves the form.

Possible formal ingredients:

- convex/concave relationships;
- pinched or tensioned curves;
- soft geometry interrupted by one precise cut;
- two or three interdependent shapes;
- negative space that carries equal visual weight to the positive form.

Avoid:

- stars;
- flowers;
- sparkles;
- pinwheels;
- clovers;
- obvious infinity marks;
- generic circles;
- app-icon blobs.

Target feeling:

**premium / modern / sculpted / quiet / specific**

---

### Territory 02 — Sharp Ornamental

**Priority: 20%**

A slightly more expressive, fashion-adjacent route.

Characteristics:

- abstract ornament;
- sharper points;
- controlled symmetry;
- emblem-like presence;
- more ceremonial than Territory 01;
- still minimal enough for UI use.

Possible formal ingredients:

- pointed geometry;
- interlocking negative spaces;
- one central axis or radial logic;
- restrained heraldic or ornamental tension without historical decoration.

Avoid:

- gothic marks;
- crests;
- crowns;
- fleur-de-lis;
- luxury-fashion clichés;
- starbursts;
- zodiac/sigil aesthetics;
- ornamental complexity that fails at favicon size.

Target feeling:

**stylish / premium / iconic / slightly fashion-adjacent**

---

### Territory 03 — Digital Abstract

**Priority: 10%**

The most software-native route, used as a counterpoint rather than the main direction.

Characteristics:

- geometric;
- systematic;
- modular;
- slightly more technical;
- capable of motion/state behavior;
- still elegant in static form.

Possible formal ingredients:

- offset modules;
- controlled discontinuity;
- paired shapes;
- optical alignment;
- one transformable relationship.

Avoid:

- AI-node diagrams;
- brackets;
- cursors;
- code symbols;
- grids;
- cubes;
- hexagons;
- crypto marks;
- generic SaaS app icons;
- cyberpunk cuts.

Target feeling:

**digital / precise / contemporary / premium**

## Wordmark studies

The symbol exploration should be paired with restrained wordmark studies.

Test at least three typographic voices:

1. refined neo-grotesk;
2. elegant humanist/hybrid sans;
3. restrained contemporary serif or semi-serif.

The wordmark should not compete with the symbol.

The symbol is the distinctive asset.

The wordmark is the calm anchor.

## Required study sheet

Each concept must show only the following:

1. symbol alone;
2. wordmark alone;
3. horizontal symbol + wordmark lockup;
4. stacked lockup;
5. favicon/social-avatar scale;
6. transparent-background favicon treatment using the emblem alone;
7. black on off-white;
8. off-white on black;
9. one neutral/silver treatment only if useful.

Do not add:

- website mockups;
- clothing mockups;
- signage;
- phones;
- fake stationery;
- billboards;
- packaging;
- fake product interfaces;
- campaign photography.

The first review is about **form quality**, not presentation quality.

## Evaluation criteria

Each concept should be judged on:

### 1. Distinctiveness
Would the emblem still be recognizable without the word Portfilo beside it?

### 2. Restraint
Does it remain premium without relying on effects, texture, or presentation?

### 3. Small-scale performance
Does it survive at favicon/social scale?

### 4. Longevity
Does it avoid looking tied to a short-lived 2026 design trend?

### 5. Portfilo fit
Can it coexist credibly with:

- software Work;
- editorial Journal;
- human Hustle;
- studio Services?

### 6. Motion potential
Can the wider identity animate around or through the emblem without requiring the mark itself to deform constantly?

### 7. Originality
Does it avoid obvious resemblance to the supplied Omen/Vivet-style reference and other common luxury/software symbols?

## Automatic rejection criteria

Reject a concept immediately if:

- it is visibly derived from P, PF, or fi;
- it resembles a sparkle/star;
- it resembles the supplied Omen emblem;
- it looks like a generic AI company;
- it looks like crypto;
- it looks like a fashion monogram;
- it requires a metallic gradient to look good;
- it only works at large size;
- its concept needs a paragraph to justify weak geometry;
- the wordmark is doing more visual work than the emblem;
- presentation polish is masking an average mark.

## Favicon boundary

The preferred favicon treatment is:

- emblem only;
- transparent background;
- no enclosing square, circle, or app-tile field by default;
- black or off-white mark selected to preserve contrast where the browser/platform permits;
- simplified geometry only if required for clarity at very small sizes.

A filled app-icon tile may still be developed later for contexts that explicitly require one, but it is not the primary favicon treatment.

## Color boundary

First-round studies are monochrome.

Use:

- black;
- off-white;
- optional neutral silver.

Do not explore brand color yet.

Color should not rescue weak geometry.

## Motion boundary

Do not animate first-round studies.

A static mark must succeed before motion is considered.

Responsive Identity behavior will be explored after one or two static identity directions survive founder review.

## Decision process

Round 1:

- 3–5 Soft Geometric marks;
- 2 Sharp Ornamental marks;
- 1–2 Digital Abstract marks;
- restrained wordmark pairings.

Founder narrows to 1–2 systems.

Round 2:

- optical refinement;
- exact proportions;
- wordmark customization;
- lockups;
- small-scale correction;
- light/dark behavior.

Round 3:

- motion/state prototype;
- favicon/social validation;
- initial website identity application.

Only after Round 3 may the homepage creative prototype begin.
