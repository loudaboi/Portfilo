# Portfilo v1 Homepage Positioning

_Last updated: 2026-09-14_

## Status

**FOUNDER-APPROVED / PHASE 3A POSITIONING AUTHORITY**

This document records the approved outcome of Phase 3A homepage positioning + narrative research.

It does not approve final public homepage copy.

## Decision job

Define the positioning territory, credible claim boundaries, and homepage narrative that Phase 3B copy must express without inventing authority.

## Approved positioning territory

**Problem-led software**

Portfilo starts with the business problem and determines the appropriate software response.

That response may include:

- custom software;
- workflow automation;
- practical AI integration;
- a customer-facing digital product where justified.

The technology or service category is not the starting point.

## Internal category

Portfilo's internal category remains:

**software design and development studio**

This remains an internal product definition and is not automatically public-facing copy.

## What the homepage must communicate immediately

The opening should make clear that:

1. Portfilo is primarily a software business, not primarily a publication, newsletter, marketing agency, or generic website shop.
2. Portfilo works from business problems rather than selling a predetermined technology stack or service menu.
3. The software response can include internal systems, automation, AI integration, and customer-facing products where justified.
4. AI is a capability within the software practice, not the master identity of the company.

## Credible claim territory

At the current stage, Portfilo may position itself around:

- solving business problems with software;
- custom software;
- workflow automation;
- practical AI integration;
- digital products where justified;
- understanding context and constraints before selecting the technical response;
- design and development as part of building the software response.

These are capability and operating-principle claims.

They do not establish client outcomes unless evidence exists for a specific project.

## Claims not currently supported

Do not claim without project-specific or company-level evidence:

- guaranteed revenue growth;
- guaranteed cost reduction;
- guaranteed efficiency gains;
- transformation at scale;
- blanket enterprise-grade delivery;
- category leadership;
- "world-class" capability;
- AI transformation expertise as the company identity;
- a large or senior multidisciplinary team;
- decades or large quantities of combined experience;
- an established proprietary methodology;
- industry specialization that has not been demonstrated;
- large-scale client relationships;
- fixed delivery-speed promises;
- measurable ROI without evidence;
- undefined long-term support or partnership models;
- uncontrollable outcome promises such as products people will love.

Avoid "full-service" as a master positioning claim.

## AI boundary

AI is an available software capability.

It should be applied where it provides a justified response to the business problem.

Portfilo should not be positioned as a generic AI agency.

## Website boundary

Website work remains valid when a website or web product is the appropriate response to a real business need.

Portfilo should not be positioned primarily as a web-development agency.

## Opening-screen clarification

Founder-approved on 2026-09-15:

The opening should communicate only:

- Portfilo identity;
- the problem-led software proposition;
- a short clarification that Portfilo designs and develops software.

It does **not** need to enumerate internal systems, automation, AI integration, and customer-facing products in the opening screen.

That capability range belongs later in Services and Work.

The opening is not required to behave like a conventional isolated marketing hero or exactly fill 100vh.

A following section may enter the initial viewport if the transition is intentional and compositionally useful.

## Homepage narrative

The approved hierarchy remains:

`Hero -> Selected Work -> Services -> Hustle -> Journal -> Newsletter -> Contact -> Footer`

Each section has a distinct narrative job.

### Hero

Establish the commercial/software proposition succinctly.

The hero should explain enough for the visitor to understand the Work that follows.

It must not attempt to prove capability through unsupported adjectives and must not overpower Work.

### Selected Work

Work is the primary proof surface.

The strongest model is:

`business context -> problem -> software response -> evidence`

Use real interfaces, workflows, system artifacts, and substantiated outcomes where available.

If proof is incomplete, do not compensate by making the hero louder or more ambitious.

### Services

Explain the range of problems Portfilo can address.

Problem territories should precede or contextualize capability labels.

Directional capability labels remain:

- Custom Software
- Automation
- AI Integration
- Digital Products

These are structure, not approved public copy.

### Hustle

Provide the human and operating-context counterweight to the studio.

Hustle should deepen Portfilo's understanding of people materially building, operating, or shaping businesses and technology.

If there is no real public Hustle piece, the homepage section must not render.

### Journal

Demonstrate technical and business thinking through durable, useful editorial work.

Journal is thinking evidence, while Work is execution evidence.

### Newsletter

Continue the audience relationship after Portfilo has established why the reader may want more.

The newsletter remains distribution and acquisition infrastructure, not the company's primary identity.

### Contact

Convert the narrative into a concrete problem conversation: what needs to improve, be replaced, automated, or built.

Final CTA wording belongs to Phase 3B.

## Approved Phase 3A decisions

1. Master positioning territory: **Problem-led software**.
2. Core distinction: Portfilo starts from the business problem, not a predetermined technology or service menu.
3. AI role: supporting capability, not master category.
4. Website-development role: valid when justified, but not master positioning.
5. Proof model: Work carries the strongest credibility burden.
6. Hero role: establish the software/commercial proposition succinctly, then yield to Work.
7. Services role: organize around problem territories first and capabilities second.
8. Editorial role: Hustle contributes human/business understanding; Journal contributes thinking; neither should obscure the studio.
9. Claim discipline: no scale, ROI, transformation, expertise, team-size, or outcome claims without evidence.
10. Empty-state rule: omit unsupported homepage sections completely; never fabricate or inflate another section to compensate for missing proof.

## Phase 3B boundary

Phase 3B may now develop and seek approval for:

- hero headline;
- hero supporting sentence;
- section framing;
- newsletter message;
- Contact CTA.

Phase 3B must derive wording from this approved territory and claim sheet.

Do not treat examples, internal labels, or directional capability names as final public copy without explicit founder approval.

Do not implement the homepage during Phase 3B.
