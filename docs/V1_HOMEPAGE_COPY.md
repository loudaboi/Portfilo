## Copy behavior

The approved homepage copy should remain restrained.

Do not add:

- unsupported quality claims;
- scale claims;
- generic agency filler;
- AI-first positioning;
- an explanatory paragraph under Selected Work;
- invented newsletter cadence;
- fabricated proof or outcomes;
- additional slogan copy merely to fill space.

Any material wording change requires a new copy decision.
