# Portfilo Public Copy Revision Candidate

_Status: candidate for user review. Not yet approved for implementation._

_Last updated: 2026-09-02_

## Governing standard

This candidate follows `docs/COPY_STYLE.md`.

Approved positioning:

> **Business and tech, from the Philippines.**

Internal voice test:

> **A smart Filipino friend who actually did the reading.**

Working principle:

> **Say what happened. Explain what matters. Don't perform intelligence.**

The visual layouts are not being reopened by this copy candidate.

## Audit summary

The current public copy has four recurring problems:

1. The retired line `Practical intelligence for ambitious Filipinos` still appears in page metadata, mastheads, heroes, positioning blocks, and footers.
2. Some brand sections use polished symmetrical slogans that describe Portfilo instead of showing the reader what Portfilo does.
3. Several paragraphs repeat `report`, `explain`, `evidence`, `useful`, and the five-topic list too often.
4. Some Subscribe copy explains editorial infrastructure when the reader mainly needs to know what arrives, how often, and how to leave.

The strongest existing copy is the literal copy on Research & Standards. That page needs only shared-brand cleanup, not a personality rewrite.

## Research & Standards `/standards`

Keep the substantive page copy as written.

Reason: methodology, corrections, AI-use, and commercial-independence copy should remain literal and restrained. Most of this page already sounds human because it describes concrete rules rather than selling the publication.

Change only shared brand copy:

- replace the retired masthead strapline;
- replace the retired footer center line.

No rewrite is proposed for:

> How Portfilo works with evidence.

> Start as close to the evidence as possible.

> Important claims have to survive a skeptical check.

> AI can help with the work. It cannot establish the facts.

> Material factual errors are corrected visibly.

> A commercial relationship cannot determine the factual conclusion.

> The evidence sets the limit.

## Story and Series templates

For this phase, change only the shared masthead/footer positioning language.

Do not rewrite story fixture headlines, descriptions, methodology, corrections, or Series fixture editorial content as part of the brand-copy revision.

The Series template still exposes `Series ID` publicly. That is implementation language and should be reviewed separately before launch, but it is not part of this copy candidate because the current phase explicitly limits Story/Series changes to shared brand copy.

## Repetition check after candidate rewrite

The full positioning line appears intentionally in limited places:

- shared masthead;
- About positioning block;
- homepage metadata/title.

It is not repeated as a homepage hero, footer slogan, newsletter promise, and About audience label.

The five-topic list remains where it performs a navigation/coverage job, but surrounding paragraphs stop repeating the same taxonomy sentence.

The words `useful`, `evidence`, `report`, and `explain` remain where they are precise rather than as recurring brand adjectives.
