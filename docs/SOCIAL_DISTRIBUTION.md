# Portfilo Audience & Social Distribution Strategy v0

_Status: FOUNDER APPROVED / CANONICAL_

_Last updated: 2026-09-06_

## Purpose

Portfilo should build an audience without turning the publication into a generic content account or a link-dump.

The operating model is:

`reporting / research / editorial thinking -> useful native social formats -> website / Portfilo Brief when deeper context is useful`

The website remains the canonical publication. Portfilo Brief is the owned audience layer. Social is primarily a discovery, explanation, and distribution layer.

Social posts do not get a weaker factual standard. Any factual claim, chart, number, quote, or interpretation must remain supportable under Portfilo's normal editorial and research rules.

## V0 channel stack

### Primary Portfilo channels

- **Facebook** - broad Philippine discovery and distribution; story posts, explainers, visual essays, charts, and fast factual posts.
- **Instagram** - primary visual-editorial channel; carousels, visual essays, data graphics, editorial imagery, and later Reels when a sustainable video workflow exists.
- **LinkedIn** - business, technology, work, startup, and professional distribution; institutional story posts, explainers, charts, and selected editorial observations.

### Personal distribution layer

- **Louda Facebook** - personal context, founder/building perspective, why a subject matters, what was learned while reporting or building, and selective sharing of Portfilo work.
- **Louda LinkedIn** - professional interpretation, founder perspective, AI/work/business observations, and selective distribution of Portfilo work.

Portfilo and Louda should not publish identical captions by default. Portfilo establishes the work; Louda provides human context and discovery.

### Core owned surfaces

- **Portfilo website** - canonical long-form publication and permanent story home.
- **Portfilo Brief** - owned returning-audience layer and newsletter relationship.

### Later channels

- **TikTok** - later, once Portfilo has a strong human-led vertical-video format worth repeating.
- **YouTube** - later, beginning with Shorts only after the same vertical-video workflow is proven; long-form video requires a separate decision.

### Deferred / no publishing obligation

- **X** - useful for monitoring, sourcing, and professional discovery when needed, but no v0 posting obligation.
- **Threads** - no distinct v0 job.
- **Reddit** - useful for listening, research leads, and genuine participation when appropriate, not as a syndication channel.

## Native Portfilo social formats

V0 begins with six institutional formats:

1. **Story Post** - a strong finding, question, or consequence from a Portfilo story; never just `new story + link`.
2. **Carousel Explainer** - normally a short sequence that explains one useful subject clearly and can stand on its own inside the feed.
3. **Editorial Visual Essay** - a multi-panel argument or observation, inspired by strong native social storytelling but executed in Portfilo's own editorial visual language.
4. **Data / Chart Post** - one number, comparison, chart, timeline, or dataset finding with source and context.
5. **Fast Factual Post** - a narrow verified update when something materially changed but does not require a full article.
6. **Editorial Observation** - a concise, supportable Portfilo interpretation or framing that gives the reader a useful way to understand a subject.

Louda's personal accounts add a separate **Founder / Personal Post** format for context, process, learning, events, and selective interpretation. That is not an institutional Portfilo content type.

## Content boundary

Portfilo social is not restricted to reporting links, but it must remain useful, specific, and evidence-aware.

Do not publish filler such as:

- generic motivation;
- daily quote graphics;
- unsupported hot takes;
- engagement bait;
- random technology memes merely to maintain cadence;
- copied headlines with no added value;
- stock-footage + AI-voice news;
- AI-generated carousel spam;
- a post simply because the account has been quiet.

A social-only explainer or observation is allowed when the job is genuinely small enough for social, but its claims must still be researched and supportable.

## Distribution model

Do not build separate content factories for Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn.

Default workflow:

`evidence / story / idea -> core social concept -> visual master -> platform adaptation`

The underlying claim, evidence, and conclusion remain consistent. Adapt the caption, crop, pacing, panel count, and call to action when the platform requires it.

The same carousel or visual essay may be adapted across Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn when the format fits. Native adaptation is preferred over blind copy-paste.

## Cadence

Use an **editorial-event cadence**, not a daily quota.

V0 target:

- roughly **2-4 meaningful Portfilo social posts per week total**, not per platform;
- a substantial story may produce roughly **2-4 useful derivatives over 7-10 days** when there are genuinely different angles;
- a fast factual update may justify only one post;
- a quiet week is acceptable when Portfilo has nothing worth saying.

This is a capacity and quality guardrail, not a growth quota.

## Visual direction

Portfilo social should feel like the publication's existing visual system translated into the feed rather than a generic social-media template pack.

Use the current brand and design system as the base:

- Instrument Sans / Source Serif 4 / IBM Plex Mono roles where appropriate;
- paper / off-white and ink / near-black foundations;
- restrained Portfilo blue and red accents;
- strong editorial typography;
- asymmetric or magazine-like composition when useful;
- whitespace, imagery, charts, source notes, figure-like details, and deliberate cropping as editorial tools.

Avoid:

- generic rounded Canva-card systems;
- gradients and glow as default decoration;
- excessive iconography;
- every slide using the same centered-text template;
- fake newspaper styling;
- decorative charts;
- generic stock-business photography;
- visual decisions copied from another publisher merely because they perform well.

The Foundr-style multi-panel idea is valid as a **format reference**, not a visual template to copy.

Before Portfilo creates reusable social templates, research the visual problem and present the system for founder approval. AI-generated visuals have no authority merely because they look plausible.

## Portfilo vs Louda

### Portfilo should usually carry

- reporting;
- evidence;
- explainers;
- charts and data;
- verified updates;
- institutional editorial analysis.

### Louda should usually carry

- why a subject caught his attention;
- what surprised him while researching or building;
- what he is learning from building Portfilo;
- event or founder context;
- personal interpretation that is clearly personal rather than institutional.

The two accounts may point to the same work, but should not normally duplicate the same copy.

## Pre-launch use

Portfilo may begin audience building before the final public domain exists, using already approved and supportable work.

Pre-launch objectives:

1. establish what Portfilo is;
2. make the editorial point of view recognizable;
3. distribute existing work;
4. learn which formats and subjects create meaningful response;
5. begin creating an audience that can later convert to the final site and Portfilo Brief.

Do not frame pre-launch posting as a large official launch if release readiness is still open.

A candidate initial sequence is:

1. Portfilo introduction through an editorial visual essay;
2. Louda's personal explanation of why he is building Portfilo;
3. `Build things that make the next thing easier` story-derived post;
4. one strong idea from that story as a visual essay;
5. `What higher-value work means in Philippine IT-BPM` story post;
6. a carousel explaining `higher-value work`;
7. Louda's personal/professional takeaway from researching Philippine IT-BPM and AI;
8. `What actually changed` story-derived post;
9. first native Portfilo editorial observation;
10. a light Louda pre-launch progress / building post.

This sequence is a planning candidate, not automatic authorization to publish the copy or visuals.

## Initial success signals

Do not judge early distribution primarily by follower count.

Useful signals include:

- shares;
- saves;
- substantive comments;
- profile visits;
- website clicks;
- newsletter subscriptions;
- repeated format or subject patterns that outperform other Portfilo posts;
- evidence that the posting workload is or is not sustainable.

Use native platform analytics plus Cloudflare Web Analytics and beehiiv initially. Do not build a custom social analytics system for v0.

## V0 non-goals

Do not add by default:

- a social-media CMS;
- scheduling infrastructure or another service before manual posting becomes a real operational problem;
- a mandatory daily content calendar;
- bulk AI content generation;
- paid social;
- engagement pods;
- growth hacks;
- a dedicated TikTok or YouTube production workflow before the video format is proven;
- dozens of rigid templates.

## Review triggers

Revisit this strategy when real distribution evidence shows that:

- one primary platform is not worth the editorial capacity it consumes;
- a deferred platform develops a clear current job;
- the visual/video workflow becomes repeatable enough to justify TikTok or YouTube;
- the audience response materially differs from the assumptions behind v0;
- Portfilo Brief or website conversion becomes the limiting growth problem;
- team capacity changes enough to support a different cadence.

Do not expand channels simply because a platform is large.
