# Portfilo Content Planning

_Last updated: 2026-09-09_

## Purpose

This document defines the lightweight v0 system Portfilo uses to decide which ideas deserve reporting time.

It does not replace:

- `EDITORIAL_STANDARD.md`;
- `RESEARCH_STANDARD.md`;
- `PUBLISHING_STANDARD.md`;
- `COPY_STYLE.md`.

Boundary:

```text
PLANNING
Signal -> Story question -> Editorial gate -> Priority -> Research assignment

RESEARCH + PUBLISHING
Research brief -> Sources -> Evidence -> Contradictions -> Story decision -> Draft -> Verification -> Publish
```

The planning system exists to improve editorial selection, not to create a larger administrative process.

## Core principle

**The unit of planning is a question, not a headline.**

Weak candidate:

> AI adoption in the Philippines

Better candidate:

> What actually prevents a small Philippine company from deploying an AI customer-support system?

The question must be specific enough to investigate without assuming the conclusion.

## Approved internal editorial focus

Portfilo remains a business and technology publication.

Approved internal editorial thesis:

> **Portfilo focuses on understanding how technology is changing companies, industries, work, and the process of building businesses, with the Philippines as its primary reporting context and AI as its strongest current acquisition and reporting wedge.**

This is internal editorial guidance, not a tagline or automatic authorization to rewrite public institutional copy.

Three priority lanes guide assignment selection:

### AI + Work

Models, products, adoption, implementation, workflows, jobs, economics, governance, and practical consequences of AI inside companies and industries.

AI is a current reporting/acquisition wedge, not Portfilo's entire identity.

### Digital Economy Infrastructure

Payments, fintech, IT-BPM, cloud, data centers, platforms, cybersecurity, connectivity, and other systems that materially enable or constrain the digital economy.

### Building Companies

Product, distribution, sales, pricing, hiring, funding, operations, business models, and decisions involved in creating and growing companies.

These strategic lanes do **not** replace the public topic taxonomy:

`Business / Technology / Startup / AI`

Strategic fit affects priority only after the normal editorial gate is passed.

Philippine reporting context should be used when geography materially affects the company, market, regulation, infrastructure, workforce, customer, or consequence being examined. Global developments can belong on Portfilo without a manufactured Philippine angle.

## Reader needs

Every candidate should identify one primary reader need.

### Update

> What changed?

Use when the main value is establishing a verified development or current state.

### Understand

> How does this work?

Use when the main value is explaining a mechanism, system, product, market, regulation, or process.

### Interpret

> What does this mean?

Use when the main value is context, consequence, tradeoffs, competing interpretations, or what established evidence suggests.

### Decide

> What should I know before making this decision?

Use for research-backed decision support without turning Portfilo into generic self-help or motivational content.

Reader need is separate from `contentType`.

Do not add more reader-need categories until publishing shows the current model is insufficient.

## Editorial gate

Before substantial research begins, a candidate should survive six gates.

### 1. Question

Is there a specific question Portfilo can actually investigate?

Reject or reframe topics that are only broad subjects, trends, or themes.

### 2. Importance

Why would the answer matter?

Importance may come from material consequences for companies, workers, customers, builders, or markets; a recurring decision; a poorly understood mechanism; a meaningful change; or a widely discussed question with weak evidence.

Do not confuse popularity with importance.

### 3. Timing

Why investigate this now?

Valid reasons include a material change, approaching decision/deadline, new evidence, a maturing market/technology, stale existing explanations, or an evergreen decision that remains poorly answered.

### 4. Contribution

What could Portfilo add that existing coverage does not already provide well?

Possible contributions include:

- original interview;
- primary-source research;
- original calculation;
- original dataset or structured sample;
- useful synthesis of fragmented evidence;
- case study;
- Philippine-specific investigation when geography genuinely affects the question;
- a useful comparison;
- a conclusion that may genuinely emerge from reporting.

Rewriting existing articles is not enough.

### 5. Evidence

Is there a credible path to evidence?

A promising question with no realistic evidence path should be held, reframed, or killed rather than converted into unsupported commentary.

### 6. Feasibility

Can Portfilo report this properly with available time, access, skills, and resources?

Consider source/interview access, data availability, technical complexity, legal/privacy concerns, expected research time, and whether the question can be scoped down without damaging its value.

Feasibility must reflect Portfilo's current reporting position, not an imagined mature newsroom.

An important but low-probability source may still be worth contacting. The assignment should not become indefinitely blocked by a reply unless that source is genuinely necessary to establish the story.

A good idea can still be the wrong assignment right now.

## Gate outcomes

Use one:

- `PASS`
- `REFRAME`
- `HOLD`
- `KILL`

Killing a weak candidate is a successful editorial outcome.

## Existing-coverage check

Before approving substantial work, ask:

> Has someone already answered this well?

Possible outcomes:

- **No:** useful information-gap signal, subject to evidence/feasibility.
- **Partly:** record what remains unanswered or weakly evidenced.
- **Yes, but circumstances changed:** record what new fact/evidence justifies another story.
- **Yes, comprehensively:** do not publish a weaker rewrite unless Portfilo can make a materially different contribution.

## Prioritization

Candidates that pass receive one priority band:

- `HIGH`
- `MEDIUM`
- `LOW`

Do not use a numerical scoring model that implies false precision.

Priority judgment may consider:

- consequence;
- information gap;
- timeliness;
- originality potential;
- evidence availability;
- Portfilo fit;
- strategic-lane fit;
- useful lifespan;
- reporting effort.

Strategic fit is a priority input, not an editorial gate.

Priority can change at any time. Time already spent is not a reason to publish.

## Candidate record

Keep the planning record compact:

```text
STORY QUESTION

Primary topic:
Business / Technology / Startup / AI

Strategic lane:
AI + Work / Digital Economy Infrastructure / Building Companies / None

Likely section:
News / Stories

Likely content type:
Reported / Explained / Guide / Profile / Analysis / Perspective / Field Notes

Primary reader need:
Update / Understand / Interpret / Decide

Why it matters:

Why now:

Possible Portfilo contribution:

Evidence path:

Access / outreach plan:

Existing coverage / information gap:

Scope / likely difficulty:

Derivative potential:
Article / newsletter / chart / social / video / other

Priority:
HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW

Decision:
PASS / REFRAME / HOLD / KILL
```

Likely section/content type are provisional. Research determines the final form.

## Copy-style integration

Planning questions should:

- use concrete nouns/verbs where possible;
- avoid hype and slogans;
- avoid curiosity gaps in place of real questions;
- avoid forcing a Philippine angle;
- avoid assuming the conclusion;
- avoid generic `future of`, `game-changing`, `revolutionary`, or similar framing when a concrete question exists;
- avoid generic AI-generated publication language.

Final headline and dek approval happens after evidence stabilizes and is governed by `COPY_STYLE.md`, `EDITORIAL_STANDARD.md`, and `EDITORIAL_PACKAGING_STANDARD.md`.

## Distribution and derivative formats

Distribution potential is not an editorial gate.

The rule remains:

**Research once, publish many.**

Newsletter, social, video, chart, carousel, and other derivatives must come from the same verified evidence position and may not introduce unsupported claims.

## Work-in-progress limits

Portfilo v0 begins with limited editorial capacity.

Default limits:

- maximum one flagship investigation in active production at once;
- generally no more than three substantial Stories in active research at once.

These are operating limits, not publishing quotas.

Do not manufacture stories to fill a schedule or homepage slots.

## Fast-moving factual updates

A short factual update may use the faster path when:

- the development is verified;
- the source is authoritative and clearly attributed;
- scope is narrow;
- Portfilo does not imply consequences that have not been researched.

`AI Update` may be used as an internal operating label for this narrow path. It is not a new public topic or `contentType`.

An AI Update may be distributed through social or the Portfilo newsletter without requiring a website story when a website page would add no meaningful value.

If the development requires explanation, testing, or consequential interpretation, create a separate candidate and use the normal gate.

## Portfilo Test

`Portfilo Test` is the approved internal format for an original product/model/agent/workflow test or comparison.

It is governed by `docs/PORTFILO_TEST_STANDARD.md` and does not bypass this planning gate. Methodology approval does not authorize a specific Test.

`AI Showcase` is not a separate evidence format. A derivative showcase may present evidence from a completed Test but may not convert casual prompting, vendor demos, one-off impressions, or cherry-picked runs into evidence.

## Editorial launch research

Launch research should determine what Portfilo investigates first rather than assuming a series or any fixed package in advance.

A candidate pool may be broad, but it is not a quota. The evidence can recommend one flagship, several unrelated strong stories, or another coherent launch package.

Public Series capability is not part of v0. If repeated real editorial inventory later justifies a Series product, that requires a new explicit product/schema/presentation decision.

## Relationship to research packets

Once a candidate becomes a substantial assignment, create the appropriate packet under `research/stories/` and follow `RESEARCH_STANDARD.md`.

For a Portfilo Test, use the Test workspace/template under `research/tests/` and `docs/PORTFILO_TEST_STANDARD.md`.

The research packet, not the planning record, becomes the source of truth for factual claims.

Planning records may contain hypotheses, leads, and provisional framing. They are not evidence.

## Review loop

Revisit this planning system only when real publishing history shows a concrete problem, such as excessive late-stage kills, reader needs that fail to describe useful work, capacity constraints, persistent prioritization failures, or multiple editors requiring clearer ownership/workflow state.

Do not expand the system in anticipation of hypothetical scale.
