# C029 Desk-Research Pilot Results

_Last updated: 2026-09-03_

## Outcome

**REFRAME / PILOT PASS / FULL CORPUS REQUIRED BEFORE STORY DECISION**

The six-event pilot supports a viable no-fieldwork version of C029, but it does not yet support an article draft or a population claim.

The method should proceed only under a narrower unit of analysis:

> **questions visible in a defined corpus of public Reddit discussion around preselected Philippine business and technology news events.**

It must not be described as what `Filipino readers`, `young Filipinos`, or `people generally` think or do.

## Pilot sample

Six recent events were locked before discussion search in `desk-pilot.md`:

- E01 — AI job displacement and retraining;
- E02 — New Clark City / Pax Silica data-center clarification;
- E03 — Oton, Iloilo proposed AI data center and water concerns;
- E04 — Meta / Roblox Philippine child-safety commitments;
- E05 — DOST P300-million AI/data-center expansion funding;
- E06 — Filipino worker AI-use / cognitive-effect survey coverage.

The sample was not replaced after discussion availability became known.

These are pilot sample counts only. They do not estimate how often news produces follow-up questions in the Philippines.

## Article-answer comparison

The most important pilot result is that public questions did **not** all have the same relationship to the source coverage.

| Relation | Pilot count |
|---|---:|
| ANSWERED | 2 |
| PARTIAL | 4 |
| UNANSWERED | 5 |
| EXPANDS_FRAME | 3 |
| Total | 14 |

This is analytically stronger than a simple `news lacks context` thesis.

### `ANSWERED`

Some questions visible in discussion were already answered by the selected source story.

Examples include:

- asking what the New Clark City project actually is after coverage explicitly distinguished advanced manufacturing from hyperscale data centers;
- asking how the Oton proposal would cool equipment after the source described its proposed hybrid cooling design.

Possible interpretations include headline-only exposure, incomplete reading, memory/attention effects, or discussion participants entering through a different information surface. The desk method cannot determine which explanation is correct.

### `PARTIAL`

Some source stories contained relevant information but did not close the question.

Examples include:

- the economic benefit of Pax Silica;
- how water would ultimately be handled in Oton;
- who financially backs the Oton proposal;
- the final Philippine policy direction around Roblox.

### `UNANSWERED`

Several questions sought operational details that the selected update did not establish.

Examples include:

- whether Iloilo utility systems could support the proposed load;
- how the Oton project would secure power;
- data-security design for Philippine identity verification;
- how age-verification bypass would be prevented.

### `EXPANDS_FRAME`

Some questions were not omissions in the narrow sense. They introduced a different but relevant frame:

- geopolitical value of Pax Silica;
- electricity pricing / cost incidence for a data center;
- household or parental accountability after platform identity verification.

This category is important because not every unanswered public question is something the original update should have answered.

## What the pilot supports

The pilot supports five methodological conclusions.

### 1. A no-fieldwork C029 is feasible

Portfilo can recover real questions without inventing them and can pair those questions with the coverage that preceded or surrounded them.

### 2. The unit of analysis must stay narrow

The evidence can establish:

> `questions appeared in these public Reddit discussions`.

It cannot establish:

> `Filipino readers generally ask these questions`.

### 3. Zero cases are essential

E01, E05 and E06 prevent the story from implying that every news event naturally generates a visible second-question trail.

### 4. The article-answer comparison is the real contribution

The strongest version of the research is not simply a taxonomy of questions.

It is the comparison:

`what the update established -> what public discussion asked -> whether the update already answered it`.

That creates contradictory and non-promotional findings by design.

### 5. The original behavioral claim must be retired for this version

Without participant observation, do not claim:

- what a person saw first;
- whether they read the full article;
- what they privately searched next;
- whether they used AI;
- why they stopped;
- how old they are;
- whether the public question represents their full information need.

The original diary/walkthrough design remains valuable as a future follow-up study, not as a prerequisite for the desk version.

## Main weaknesses exposed by the pilot

### Platform bias

Question visibility depends heavily on whether a story is discussed on Reddit and which subreddit receives it.

E03 was unusually discussion-rich because it involved a contentious local consultation. E06 had a direct story-share post but produced no usable questions.

### Event-matching ambiguity

Some ongoing stories, especially Pax Silica and Roblox regulation, unfold through multiple updates. A later thread may respond to a new implementation detail rather than the exact article selected for comparison.

The full corpus therefore needs a fixed time window and explicit event-match rule.

### Search discoverability

Accessible web search cannot guarantee exhaustive enumeration of every relevant Reddit thread.

The full method must describe the corpus as located through a documented search procedure rather than as all discussion that existed.

### Topic concentration

Three of six pilot events concern AI/data-center infrastructure and two concern AI/work. This is acceptable for feasibility testing but not for the final corpus.

### Commenter representativeness

Reddit commenters are self-selected and are not a representative sample of the Philippine public.

This limitation is structural and cannot be repaired through a larger Reddit sample. The article must remain about the corpus, not the population.

## Recommended full-corpus design

If C029 proceeds, use a Reddit-only corpus for methodological consistency rather than mixing platforms with different ranking, identity and comment systems.

### Event sample

Target `24` preselected Philippine business/technology news events over a fixed recent window.

Use four clusters with six events each:

1. `AI / work / enterprise adoption`;
2. `companies / startups / markets`;
3. `fintech / platforms / regulation`;
4. `infrastructure / data centers / semiconductors`.

Select events before discussion search and preserve zero-result cases.

### Source diversity

Do not let one publisher or one recurring controversy dominate the event sample.

Prefer a mix of credible Philippine and international reporting where the Philippine event is central.

### Discussion window

Use a fixed `0-7 day` window from the selected source publication date for the primary corpus.

A later discussion may be retained only as a separately labeled follow-up and should not be mixed into the primary counts.

### Thread matching

Require one of:

- direct link to the selected article;
- exact event/headline match;
- unmistakable response to the same announcement, decision or incident inside the seven-day window.

If the match is ambiguous, exclude it.

### Extraction

For each qualifying thread:

- preserve the URL and date;
- record whether it directly links the source article;
- extract only genuine information-seeking questions;
- exclude rhetoric, insults, jokes and duplicate questions;
- code the question;
- code article relation as `ANSWERED / PARTIAL / UNANSWERED / EXPANDS_FRAME / UNCLEAR`;
- retain a short audit note explaining the relation decision.

### Counts

Sample counts may be published only as descriptions of the defined corpus, for example:

> `In Portfilo's 24-event sample...`

Never convert those counts into claims such as `X% of Filipino readers`.

## Story possibilities after the full corpus

The final story should be allowed to emerge from the data.

Possible outcomes include:

### A — Mechanism gap

If mechanism questions remain common across beats and are frequently unresolved, the story may examine the gap between announcement-level updates and operational explanation.

### B — The article already answered it

If many public questions are already answered by the article, the more interesting story may concern fragmented/headline-led news exposure rather than journalism failing to explain.

The desk method cannot establish why people missed the information, so this would require careful framing.

### C — Frame expansion

If discussion repeatedly moves into consequences, comparison and responsibility, the story may show how public information needs expand beyond the narrow job of an initial update.

### D — No stable pattern

If the full corpus is dominated by topic/platform noise, keep C029 held or kill this desk version.

## Pilot decision

**PILOT PASS.**

C029 no longer needs to be treated as intrinsically fieldwork-dependent.

However, the pilot is not enough to reactivate it as a publishable story or displace C021 automatically.

Recommended planning state:

**`REFRAME / PILOT PASS / FULL CORPUS REQUIRED`**

The original fieldwork version remains preserved for future use.

No article draft should be started.
