# C029 Research Brief

_Last updated: 2026-09-03_

## Status

**HOLD / FUTURE AUDIENCE RESEARCH / LAUNCH DESK METHOD REJECTED**

C029 began as an attempt to investigate the information problem underneath Portfilo's reason for existing.

Its strongest original question remains behavioral:

> **What questions do younger Filipino readers ask after first encountering a business or technology news story, and what do they do next to answer them?**

That question is still editorially interesting, but answering it properly requires original reader observation. Portfilo does not currently have the recruitment reach to execute the planned fieldwork without an overly narrow sample.

The research is preserved for later rather than weakened.

## Original contribution

The intended contribution is the observed sequence:

`encounter -> unanswered question -> next action -> stopping point`

A future study may use the preserved method in `fieldwork-plan.md`:

- `12-15` Filipino adults aged 18-34;
- minimum `10` complete records before a publish decision;
- three-day natural news-encounter diary;
- semi-structured interview;
- real-time walkthrough;
- AI users and non-users;
- negative cases including no follow-up;
- no population-level prevalence claims from the qualitative sample.

## Desk-pilot history

Because participant recruitment was not feasible at launch, Portfilo tested a narrower desk method using a preselected six-event public-discussion corpus.

Pilot files remain preserved:

- `desk-pilot.md`;
- `desk-pilot-questions.csv`;
- `desk-pilot-results.md`.

The pilot demonstrated that public follow-up questions could be recovered and compared with what a selected source article already established. It also produced a useful contradiction: a follow-up question does not necessarily mean the source story failed to explain something; some questions were already answered, some partly answered, some unresolved, and some expanded the frame.

However, Louda Tuppal rejected a Reddit-led corpus as the evidentiary backbone for a Portfilo launch article. That editorial judgment is now locked for C029's launch use.

Do not proceed with the previously proposed 24-event Reddit corpus as Story #3.

The pilot remains research history and may still inform future methodology or lead generation. It is not publication evidence for a current article.

## Why C029 is no longer the mission article

The attempt to make C029 carry Portfilo's launch mission created unnecessary research burden and kept forcing the story to prove claims about readers.

The publication's mission does not require Portfilo to establish that readers are confused, that journalism lacks context, or that young Filipinos follow a particular information-seeking path.

A separate founding Perspective, C030, now carries that job through an explicit editorial argument about what Portfilo chooses to follow across Business, Technology, Startups, AI, and Building.

C030 packet:

`research/stories/what-actually-changed/`

C029 should therefore be judged later on its own merits as audience/news-behavior research, not as institutional justification for Portfilo.

## Revisit trigger

Resume C029 when Portfilo can recruit at least 10 suitable participants without relying almost entirely on Louda Tuppal's immediate friend/founder/supporter circle, or when another professional primary-data route can answer the behavioral question to the same standard.

## What not to claim

Do not use the existing C029 packet to claim:

- that most Filipino readers need a second explanation;
- that young Filipinos are confused by business or technology news;
- that Philippine journalism broadly lacks context;
- that AI is the normal second step after encountering news;
- that Reddit commenters represent Filipino readers;
- that Portfilo exists because other publications fail.
