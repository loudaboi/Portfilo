# C027 Draft v1 Review

_Last updated: 2026-09-03_

## Decision

**REJECT / DO NOT POLISH**

Draft v1 is preserved as a failed concept draft for editorial history. It should not be used as the base for publication.

## Reader feedback that caused the reset

The draft felt like it was going in several directions at once. The main message was hard to identify, and the article appeared to force multiple answers from an earlier discovery conversation into one story.

That diagnosis is accepted.

## Editorial diagnosis

The draft tried to carry all of these as co-equal ideas:

- starting without much leverage;
- bottleneck migration;
- AI narrowing some capability gaps;
- skills;
- habits;
- networks;
- trust and reputation;
- understanding users;
- preparation versus action.

Each can be relevant to `Building`, but relevance is not a reason to put them in one essay. Their inclusion weakened the organizing idea.

## Rule for the replacement draft

Do not ask, `Which of the user's answers can fit into this article?`

Ask, `What is the one idea this article is trying to leave with the reader?`

Material belongs only if it advances that idea.

See `topic-structure-research.md` and `draft-v2.md` for the reset.
