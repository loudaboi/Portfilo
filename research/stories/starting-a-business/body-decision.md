## Approved body

Source: `draft-v8.md`

The founder/editor approved Draft 8 as the body direction on 2026-09-07.

The approved article explains one practical problem for a founder, small-business owner, or reader learning about business:

> A bad business result tells you what happened. It does not automatically tell you what caused it.

The article develops that distinction through weak sales, shows why different possible causes imply different responses, explains that wrong diagnosis can waste limited resources, and gives one conceptual response without becoming a comprehensive guide:

> When the cause is unclear, the next action should ideally help the owner learn which explanation is more likely before committing more resources.

## Public-assembly opening trim

When the story is assembled for the public content entry, omit the first two sentences from `draft-v8.md`:

> What actually makes starting a business difficult, beyond the workload?
>

Those sentences were useful before the final editorial package existed, but the approved headline and dek now perform those jobs. Keeping them would create unnecessary headline/dek/body repetition.

The canonical public body therefore begins:

> **Low sales are an easy example. They tell the owner that not enough sales happened. They do not say whether the price is wrong, the offer is weak, the wrong people are seeing it, too few people know about it, or demand is simply lower than expected.**

No other sentence in Draft 8 is reopened or changed by this trim.
