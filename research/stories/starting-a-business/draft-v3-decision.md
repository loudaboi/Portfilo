# Draft 3 Decision — Starting a Business

_Last updated: 2026-09-07_

## Status

**REJECTED / DO NOT USE AS WRITING PRECEDENT**

## Founder-editor decision

Draft 3 was rejected because simplifying the prose and reducing citations did not fix the deeper problem: the article structure was still AI-invented.

The draft used a clean multi-section progression that appeared reasonable but did not have sufficient structural authority from documented professional practice or a matching human-edited exemplar.

## What specifically failed

- the section architecture was selected by the model rather than earned by the material;
- the explanatory progression was too neat and visibly synthetic;
- the draft still felt generated even after research density was reduced;
- the body treated `less citation` as if it automatically meant `better explainer`;
- the structural form was not re-researched after repeated draft failure, despite `docs/STYLE_RESEARCH_STANDARD.md` requiring re-research when a draft fights the selected form.

## What remains usable

The subject research packet remains valid unless separately contradicted or superseded.

The founder-approved editorial direction also remains valid:

- the story is `Explained`;
- the primary reader need is `Understand`;
- the purpose is to help the reader understand the difficulty of starting a business without reducing it to `grind harder`, `most people give up`, or `never quit`;
- research should support the explanation rather than become the visible structure of the article.

## Required correction

Before another draft:

1. re-research the professional Explainer form;
2. review human-edited exemplars that solve the same information problem;
3. identify structural options whose authority comes from those sources and the actual evidence shape;
4. obtain founder-editor approval of the selected structural method;
5. revise `style-brief.md` to remove the rejected AI-invented architecture;
6. obtain founder-editor approval of the revised brief;
7. only then create a new draft.
