# Starting a Business Draft 2 Decision

_Last updated: 2026-09-07_

## Status

**DRAFT 2 REJECTED / WRITING DIRECTION SUPERSEDED**

## Decision

The founder/editor rejected `draft-v2.md` as an acceptable Portfilo Explained direction.

The rejection is editorial, not evidentiary. The underlying research packet remains usable.

Main reasons:

- the opening retained AI-shaped, fake-profound phrasing;
- the body exposed too much of the research packet;
- source names, studies, and citations became part of the visible structure instead of staying mostly backstage;
- the draft read like a synthesis of research rather than an explanation for the audience;
- the article tried to include too many findings simply because they had been researched;
- the prose optimized for sounding researched instead of making the subject easy to understand.

This decision supersedes the `FOUNDER-EDITOR REVIEW REQUIRED` status inside `draft-v2.md`.

Do not line-edit Draft 2 into publication.

## Replacement direction

Rewrite from the verified research packet using `docs/EXPLAINED_STANDARD.md`.

The next draft should:

- explain one central idea in ordinary language;
- keep most source machinery backstage;
- use only the evidence required for comprehension, accuracy, scale, contradiction, or useful Philippine context;
- avoid literature-review structure;
- avoid famous-founder name accumulation;
- avoid fake profundity, punchline contrasts, and AI-shaped connective prose;
- remain an `Explained` story, not Analysis, Perspective, Guide, or motivational founder content.

## Existing published stories

The founder/editor also raised a separate concern that Portfilo's earlier published launch stories may reflect the older writing process.

No deletion or unpublishing is authorized by this decision.

Those stories should receive a separate audit against the current research/writing standards before any retain/revise/unpublish/delete decision.
