The article shape was still being invented by AI from the research packet: neat conceptual sections, balanced mechanisms, and a final synthesis. That violated `docs/STYLE_RESEARCH_STANDARD.md`, which requires professional form research and human-edited exemplars to establish defensible structural options before drafting.

This pass does **not** write a new article. Its job is to identify a proven explainer form that actually matches this story's reader need and evidence shape.

## Reader job

Primary need: `Understand`.

The reader should leave understanding why starting a business can be difficult without reducing the answer to:

- long hours;
- `grind harder`;
- `most people give up`;
- `never quit`;
- one universal startup-failure formula.

The story has one main information problem rather than several unrelated questions.

## Professional form guidance

### Poynter — `How journalists can create better explainers`

URL: https://www.poynter.org/newsletters/2013/how-journalists-can-create-better-explainers/

Observed professional practice:

Poynter reports Slate chief Explainer Brian Palmer's working method as:

1. start with a question;
2. follow with a succinct answer;
3. elaborate and add necessary peripheral detail.

Palmer also emphasizes that the question and answer should be understandable without requiring background knowledge the reader is unlikely to have.

Transferable decision:

For a story with one genuine explanatory question, the article does not need an AI-invented multi-section architecture before it begins. The reader can receive the question, the answer, and then the explanation needed to understand why that answer is true.

Boundary:

This is a documented professional method, not permission to copy Slate's voice, humor, cadence, or headline style.

### Poynter — `10 tips for making hard facts easy reading` / `How to make hard facts easy to read`

URLs:

- https://www.poynter.org/educators-students/2016/10-tips-for-making-hard-facts-easy-reading/
- https://www.poynter.org/reporting-editing/2020/how-to-make-hard-facts-easy-to-read/

Observed professional practice:

For difficult information, Roy Peter Clark recommends slowing the rate of information, using few numbers, shortening sentences and paragraphs at points of complexity, uncluttering the lead, translating jargon, and cutting unnecessary information.

Transferable decision:

Research volume must not determine article density. Complexity should cause the prose to slow down, not cause more studies, names, statistics, and qualifications to be displayed at once.

Boundary:

These are clarity tools, not a fixed prose template.

### Pulitzer Prize — Explanatory Reporting criteria

URLs:

- https://www.pulitzer.org/prize-winners-by-category/207
- https://www.pulitzer.org/node/2026-journalism-submission-guidelines-requirements-and-faqs

Observed professional criterion:

Explanatory reporting is judged on whether it illuminates a significant and complex subject through mastery of the subject, lucid writing, and clear presentation. The submission guidance further says the main thrust must genuinely be explanatory and provide deeper understanding.

Transferable decision:

The visible article should demonstrate subject mastery through clarity and selection. It does not need to display the research process to prove that the research happened.

## Human-edited exemplar 1 — Slate Explainer

Publication: Slate

Column: Explainer

Article: `It's 11:48 a.m. Do You Know Where Your Missile Is?`

Author: Brian Palmer

Archive source: https://www.slate.com/media/70/04102009.pdf

Why this exemplar matters:

It is an actual example by the journalist whose explainer method Poynter documents.

Observed opening behavior:

- establishes the immediate situation;
- states the precise question the reader needs answered;
- answers it immediately with a short direct answer;
- then explains the mechanics and limitations that make the answer true.

Observed structural behavior:

The article does not begin by dividing the subject into a symmetrical set of mechanisms. It follows the information problem. Detail is added because the answer needs explanation, not because the writer needs a predetermined number of sections.

Transferable decision:

A direct question-and-answer spine is a proven form for a single explanatory problem.

Do not imitate:

- Slate's headline style;
- Palmer's cadence;
- humor;
- any sentence-level wording.

## Human-edited exemplar 2 — Reuters formal explainer

Publication: Reuters

Article: `EXPLAINER Tough EU tech rules: here's the what, why and what comes next`

Author: Foo Yun Chee

URL: https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/explainer-tough-eu-tech-rules-heres-what-why-what-comes-next-2020-12-15/

Observed structural behavior:

The piece orients the reader to the development, then uses literal question sections such as:

- what are the rules;
- why now;
- what comes next;
- what different parties think.

The section structure exists because the subject contains several separable reader questions.

Transferable decision:

Modular Q&A is a proven explainer form when a subject genuinely contains multiple independent questions.

Why it is not the primary fit here:

The starting-a-business story currently has one main question and one connected explanation. Forcing `what / why / what next` or another multi-question grid onto it would recreate the same artificial structure problem in a different form.

Do not imitate:

Reuters' wire cadence, all-caps headings, sentence rhythm, or institutional voice.

Publication: Harvard Business Review

Article: `Why Start-ups Fail`

Author: Tom Eisenmann

Observed structural basis:

Its taxonomy, personal-author framing, wording, or rhetorical devices.

## Structural options established by the research

### Option A — Direct question -> succinct answer -> explanation

Authority:

Poynter-documented Slate Explainer method plus actual Slate Explainer exemplar.

Motion:

1. establish the real reader question with only the context needed to understand it;
2. give the clearest defensible answer quickly;
3. explain why that answer is true in the order the reader needs;
4. introduce evidence, examples, definitions, or counterevidence only when the explanation requires them;
5. stop when the answer is understood.

Sectioning:

No predetermined section count.

Subheads may appear only if the developing explanation becomes long enough that navigation materially helps the reader. They must emerge from actual reader questions or turns in the explanation, not from a need for visual symmetry.

Assessment:

**RECOMMENDED FOR THIS STORY.**

Why:

The assignment has one central explanatory problem and the evidence supports one connected answer. This form provides an externally grounded spine without inventing a taxonomy the reporting did not produce.

### Option B — Modular Q&A

Authority:

Reuters formal Explainer practice.

Motion:

Break the subject into several literal reader questions and answer each separately.

Assessment:

**VALID FORM / NOT RECOMMENDED FOR CURRENT STORY.**

Why:

The current article does not naturally contain several independent questions. Using this structure would risk manufacturing them.

### Option C — Research-derived taxonomy / pattern explainer

Authority:

HBR example when backed by the author's own research program.

Assessment:

**REJECT FOR THIS STORY.**

Why:

The Portfilo research packet does not establish a validated taxonomy of startup hardship or failure. A numbered or named framework would therefore be an editorial invention rather than a reported finding.

## Evidence-display decision under Option A

The article should not be planned around a target number of citations or sources.

Instead, evidence becomes visible when the reader needs it to:

- trust a consequential factual claim;
- understand scale;
- see why a familiar assumption is misleading;
- understand a Philippine-specific constraint that changes the explanation;
- understand the persistence-versus-exit contradiction.

The rest of the source base remains in the research packet.

This is consistent with Poynter's clarity guidance: difficult material should be slowed down and unnecessary information cut rather than packed into the prose.
