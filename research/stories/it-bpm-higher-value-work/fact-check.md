Decision: **PASS**

- does not imply a wage premium;
- uses the industry phrase while the body immediately explains that it is not an official occupation category.

### Dek

Decision: **PASS**

### F13 - Sample size and representativeness

Claim:

The analysis uses 15 purposefully selected employer postings and is not a prevalence sample.

Research source: `sample.md`.  
Status: **VERIFIED METHOD STATEMENT**

Required treatment:

Do not derive percentages, hiring shares, growth rates or sector composition from the sample.

Claim:

The sample does not establish a wage premium because employer-primary salary disclosure is not sufficiently comparable and broad wage data do not map cleanly onto the role taxonomy.

Research source: E14, X12-X13, `notes.md`.  
Status: **VERIFIED RESEARCH LIMITATION**
