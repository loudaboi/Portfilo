OECD's 2026 Philippines survey describes IT-BPM employment as concentrated in contact centres/back-office operations while the sector has diversified into GCCs, healthcare information management and IT/software.

Research source: S001 / E01  
Current verification: OECD page rechecked 2026-09-03.

Pre-publish treatment:

The exact `approximately 1.8 million workers` figure was removed from the article because a newer IBPAP-outlook report refers to 1.9 million employees as of 2025. The article does not need to resolve that noncentral difference.

### F02 - IBPAP capacity-to-capability strategy

Claim:

IBPAP's 2028 outlook says the industry's next phase should rely less on workforce scale and increasingly on higher-value capabilities and AI-enabled talent, with revenue per worker becoming a more important competitiveness measure.

Research source: S025 primary; S009 independent cross-check / E16  
Current verification: IBPAP official article and Newsbytes rechecked 2026-09-03.  
Status: **VERIFIED AS IBPAP STRATEGY / CLAIM**

Public-copy treatment:

Attribute the strategy to IBPAP. Do not convert it into proof of realized productivity, wages, employment composition or worker mobility.
