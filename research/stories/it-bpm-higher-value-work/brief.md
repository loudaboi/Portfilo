## Research question

> What does "higher-value work" actually mean inside Philippine IT-BPM?

This is a research question, not a headline or accepted industry conclusion.

## Reader payoff

Turn a recurring sector phrase into concrete evidence about actual roles, tasks, skill requirements, compensation, and access. The story should help readers understand what the industry's stated shift means in labor-market terms rather than repeating generic claims that AI will create higher-skill jobs.

## Working hypothesis

**Hypothesis, not conclusion:** The phrase `higher-value work` currently groups together several materially different job families, and the real transition can be understood only by defining those roles and comparing their tasks, skills, compensation, and hiring requirements with more routine service work.

## Disproof condition

Reframe if employers and official/industry sources use the term too inconsistently to support a defensible classification, or if available job-posting and compensation evidence cannot distinguish the claimed role categories without arbitrary assumptions.

## Scope

Primary geography: Philippines.  
Primary period: current hiring and role evidence from 2025-2026, with older context only where necessary.

Before building any job-posting dataset, define:

- included job families;
- operational definition of `higher-value`;
- source/job-board universe;
- collection window;
- duplicate handling;
- location and remote/hybrid treatment;
- compensation fields and their limitations.

Advertised salary must not be presented as realized compensation.

## Potential evidence

- employer career pages and job listings;
- job boards as Tier C market evidence;
- IBPAP, TESDA, DICT, PSA, BSP, and OECD material where relevant;
- recruiters and HR leaders;
- workers who have moved between role types;
- compensation evidence with methodology caveats;
- job-task and skill descriptions.
