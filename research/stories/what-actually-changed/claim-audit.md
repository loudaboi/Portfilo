## Editorial-judgment statements

Statements such as these are treated as argument/editorial judgment rather than measured empirical findings:

- business and technology do not remain inside neat editorial categories;
- software can change company operations and thereby affect costs, jobs, products, or markets;
- capital shapes what a startup can attempt and how long it can continue;
- technology becomes a different operating question when it enters a real workflow;
- building something brings product, customer, pricing, hiring, capital, regulation, and infrastructure constraints together.

These claims are broad, ordinary descriptions of how the subjects relate. They are not presented with numerical prevalence, causal precision, or claims of universal application.
