### Revision 1 — `same set of decisions` is too totalizing

Current sentence:

`Portfilo covers Business, Technology, Startups, AI, and Building because developments in those areas can change the same set of decisions.`

Problem:

The phrase creates cleaner conceptual unity than the essay needs or can literally defend. Different stories affect overlapping decisions, not necessarily one stable `same set`.

Required revision:

Describe the subjects as affecting one another through concrete company/product/operating decisions without inventing a single unified decision set.

### Revision 2 — reduce polished symmetry in the ending

Current ending:

`If those additional facts materially change ... they belong in the story. If they do not, the update can stand on its own.`

Problem:

The mirrored construction is concise but reads like a finished editorial maxim. C030's style brief specifically rejects slogan-like closure.

Required revision:

End in ordinary explanatory prose, retaining the conditional standard without a matched two-part formula.

### Revision 3 — simplify the paragraph-8 three-part payoff

Current sentence:

`Sometimes it simply clarifies a term, narrows a claim, or shows that an announcement has not yet changed much beyond the announcement itself.`

Problem:

The three-part sequence is not wrong, but together with the conclusion it creates a visibly composed cadence.

Required revision:

Use one or two concrete outcomes rather than a rhetorical three-part completion.
