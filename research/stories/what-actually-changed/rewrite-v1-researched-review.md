### Revision 1 — remove meta-editorial headline paragraph

Current paragraph begins:

`That standard also puts a limit on the question in the working title.`

Problem:

This sounds like an internal memo and ties the body to an unsettled headline. It also repeats the exact working-title phrase despite the approved brief warning against turning it into a refrain/framework.

Required revision:

Preserve only the substantive limitation: the extra question is conditional and should not become a ritual applied to every development.

### Revision 2 — remove the `shallow and deep journalism` contrast

Current sentence:

`The distinction is therefore not between shallow and deep journalism.`

Problem:

Even though the sentence rejects the binary, it introduces an unnecessary quality hierarchy and media-industry frame. C030 is not an argument about whether other journalism is shallow.

Required revision:

State the distinction directly in terms of whether additional implementation/economic/contextual information materially changes understanding.

### Revision 3 — compress the Building paragraph slightly

The Building paragraph currently stacks product idea, pricing, software/process, distribution, hiring, regulation/payment flow, and topic-boundary language.

Problem:

The examples are valid but the accumulation begins to recreate the old draft's long Building section.

Required revision:

Keep only the connections necessary to show that building forces technology/business decisions into concrete constraints.
