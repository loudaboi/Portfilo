# TikTok Commerce Philippines Evidence Ledger

_Last updated: 2026-09-11_

## C01 - TikTok Shop is already a major Philippine e-commerce platform

Candidate claim:
TikTok is no longer a marginal shopping add-on in the Philippines.

Evidence:
Momentum Works estimated 2025 Philippine platform e-commerce GMV at US$21.1B, with:
- Shopee: 49%
- TikTok Shop: 29%
- Lazada: 22%

Portfilo calculation:
US$21.1B x 29% = approximately **US$6.12B** in TikTok Shop GMV under Momentum Works' methodology.

Source:
S01

Tier:
B

Confidence:
HIGH for the reported estimate and calculation.

Caveat:
Momentum Works estimates GMV and includes paid orders that may later be cancelled, returned or refunded. GMV is not TikTok revenue or net completed consumer spending.

Status:
VERIFIED / QUALIFY METHODOLOGY

## C02 - Philippine consumers are demonstrably using TikTok to shop

Candidate claim:
TikTok is not only a content platform where products are advertised; Filipinos report buying through it.

Evidence:
Rakuten Insight's February 2025 Philippine social-commerce survey covered 7,630 respondents aged 16+ who had made social-media purchases. In the accessible summary, TikTok ranked as the most popular social-media platform used for purchases in the previous 12 months.

Source:
S04

Tier:
C

Confidence:
MEDIUM-HIGH

Caveat:
The sample is limited to social-commerce purchasers, not all Filipinos or all internet users. Exact percentage is not available in the accessible result.

Status:
VERIFIED / QUALIFY SAMPLE

## C03 - Content commerce has become structurally important across Southeast Asia

Candidate claim:
TikTok Shop is benefiting from a broader shift in which video and creator content generate a meaningful share of e-commerce transactions.

Evidence:
Momentum Works estimates content commerce at US$49.7B in Southeast Asia in 2025, or 32% of platform e-commerce GMV, up from 20% in 2024.

Google / Temasek / Bain separately estimate video commerce at about 25% of total e-commerce GMV in 2025 and describe growth as driven by high volumes of lower-value transactions, local creators and social-to-e-commerce integration.

Source:
S01, S02, S03

Tier:
B

Confidence:
HIGH for direction; MEDIUM for exact cross-source comparison.

Caveat:
The reports use different definitions and denominators. Do not present 25% and 32% as directly comparable estimates of the same metric.

Status:
VERIFIED / QUALIFY DEFINITIONS

## C04 - TikTok directly turns creators into sales channels

Candidate claim:
TikTok Shop's creator ecosystem is commercially wired into transactions rather than functioning only as advertising.

Evidence:
Official Philippine TikTok Shop documentation states that creators can earn commission on qualifying product sales attributed through:
- LIVE;
- short video;
- Showcase;
- Affiliate Links.

Seller documentation allows merchants to set commission rates for creator affiliates.

Source:
S10, S11, S12

Tier:
A

Confidence:
HIGH

Caveat:
This establishes the incentive architecture, not how much creator content causes total Philippine GMV.

Status:
VERIFIED

## C05 - TikTok reduces the gap between content discovery and purchase

Candidate claim:
TikTok Shop lets the same content surface function as discovery, recommendation and transaction entry point.

Evidence:
TikTok Shop's affiliate and seller documentation connects shoppable short video, LIVE, product Showcase, recommendations and checkout attribution inside one commerce system.

Google / Temasek / Bain describe seamless social-to-e-commerce integration as one factor behind the regional rise of video commerce.

Source:
S03, S10, S11

Tier:
A/B

Confidence:
HIGH

Caveat:
"Seamless" is partly descriptive language from industry/platform sources. The article should explain the actual steps rather than rely on the adjective.

Status:
VERIFIED

## C06 - Discounts and subsidies are part of the growth mechanism

Candidate claim:
TikTok Shop does not rely on content alone; it financially reduces purchase friction.

Evidence:
TikTok Shop Philippines currently operates:
- shipping-fee support;
- discount vouchers;
- cashback;
- flash-sale subsidies;
- platform-funded shipping and product promotions;
- co-funded Mega Discount programs.

Current Mega Discount documentation advertises TikTok Shop-sponsored discounts of up to 70% across LIVE, Short Video and Shop Tab.

Source:
S13, S14, S15

Tier:
A

Confidence:
HIGH

Caveat:
Current program terms do not establish the exact subsidy level or causal contribution during every earlier growth period.

Status:
VERIFIED / QUALIFY CAUSAL WEIGHT

Candidate claim:
Commerce participation on TikTok Shop is growing alongside transaction volume.

TikTok reports:
- local seller sales up more than 200% in 2025;
- active TikTok Shop creators doubled year on year by May 2026;
- July 2025 to July 2026 orders in Visayas and Mindanao up 140%, sellers up 50%, creators up 160%.

S07, S08, S09

## C08 - Social-commerce convenience matters, but trust remains a constraint

Candidate claim:
The same format that reduces shopping friction also creates a trust problem that platforms and regulators are trying to address.

Evidence:
Rakuten Insight's accessible Philippine survey summary says convenience was the leading stated reason for social-commerce purchases.

Among non-buyers, preference for official websites or physical stores and lack of trust in the social-media buying process were common reasons not to purchase.

DTI launched the E-Commerce Philippine Trustmark in 2025 to strengthen safety, reliability and consumer confidence in online transactions.

Source:
S05, S06, S19

Tier:
A/C

Confidence:
MEDIUM-HIGH

Caveat:
These are broad social-commerce findings, not TikTok-only buyer motivations.

Status:
VERIFIED / QUALIFY SCOPE

## C09 - Philippine business participation extends beyond creator side hustles

Candidate claim:
TikTok Shop is being used by established businesses as well as individual creators and micro-sellers.

Evidence:
BusinessWorld documented established Philippine businesses entering TikTok Shop. Mega Prime said it expanded into TikTok Shop in 2023 and reported higher product reviews and sales during special promotions.

DTI entered a formal MSME training partnership with TikTok Shop in 2025.

Source:
S17, S18

Tier:
A/B

Confidence:
HIGH for participation; LOW-MEDIUM for generalizing seller outcomes.

Caveat:
Individual company results are examples, not market averages.

Status:
VERIFIED / QUALIFY GENERALIZATION

## Current evidence decision

The core question survives.

The strongest evidence does **not** support a one-factor explanation such as:
- Filipinos trust creators more;
- TikTok is simply cheaper;
- live selling alone caused the shift.

It does support a layered commerce mechanism:
- discovery inside entertainment;
- creator affiliate incentives;
- shoppable video and LIVE;
- integrated transaction paths;
- platform-funded discounts;
- a Philippine market already comfortable with mobile/social commerce.

Research outcome:
**PUBLISH**
