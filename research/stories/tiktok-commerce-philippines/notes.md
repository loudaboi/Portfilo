# TikTok Commerce Philippines Research Notes

_Last updated: 2026-09-11_

## Current working answer

Do not treat this as article copy.

TikTok Shop appears to have become a serious Philippine shopping channel because it changed where the shopping funnel begins.

Traditional marketplace behavior often begins with intent:
- open a shopping app;
- search for a product;
- compare listings;
- purchase.

TikTok can begin earlier:
- watch a video or LIVE;
- encounter a product;
- see it demonstrated or recommended;
- tap the product;
- receive a voucher/shipping incentive;
- check out.

The platform then gives creators a direct financial reason to create more selling content through affiliate commissions.

This creates a feedback loop:
more creators and sellers -> more product content -> more discovery opportunities -> more transactions -> more incentive to produce commerce content.

The evidence supports that mechanism, but not a precise causal ranking.

## Useful calculations

Momentum Works 2025 Philippines estimate:
- platform e-commerce GMV: US$21.1B
- TikTok Shop share: 29%
- derived TikTok Shop GMV: **US$6.119B**, rounded to **about US$6.1B**

Formula:
21.1 x 0.29 = 6.119

Do not call this revenue or net sales.

## Strongest article spine

A concise draft could likely be built around four sections:

1. **TikTok Shop is already large enough to be a real marketplace**
   - 29% of estimated Philippine platform e-commerce GMV in 2025.
   - Rakuten survey ranks TikTok first among surveyed social-commerce purchasers.

2. **The storefront moved into the feed**
   - short video and LIVE make product discovery part of entertainment.
   - regional video/content commerce data show this is a structural e-commerce shift.

3. **Creators became a distributed sales force**
   - affiliate commissions connect creator content directly to sales.
   - make the commercial incentive explicit.

4. **Content is only half the system**
   - vouchers, shipping support, cashback and other subsidies reduce friction.
   - trust remains a constraint.
   - do not turn the story into praise for TikTok.

Target:
**700-900 words**, preferably closer to the lower end if the answer remains clear.

## Potential optional outreach

### TikTok Shop Philippines
Ask:
- Philippine GMV/order growth by content surface if disclosable;
- share of sales attributed to affiliate creators;
- LIVE vs short-video vs Shop Tab contribution;
- definitions behind local seller sales growth.

### Seller / brand
Ask:
- what percentage of TikTok Shop orders arrive through affiliates or content rather than direct store/search;
- how much vouchers/platform support matter to conversion;
- how creator commissions are set.

### Creator
Ask:
- how products are selected;
- how commission affects what they promote;
- short video vs LIVE performance;
- whether repeat buyers later bypass creator content.

These interviews are optional for the current desk explainer.
