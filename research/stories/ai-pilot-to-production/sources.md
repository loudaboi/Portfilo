# C001 Source Inventory - AI Pilot to Production

_Last updated: 2026-09-02_

## Source policy

This file is a working source inventory under `docs/RESEARCH_STANDARD.md`.

Tier labels describe the type of evidence, not automatic truthfulness. A company or vendor source can be Tier A evidence that the organization made a claim while still requiring independent verification of the underlying result.

Access date for sources below: 2026-09-02 unless otherwise noted.

## S001 - The Philippine AI Report 2025, full version

**Source:** Swarm Technologies, Inc.  
**URL:** https://www.swarm.work/philippine-ai-report-full  
**Type / tier:** Industry survey / Tier C  
**Data/report period:** 2025 report; survey describes prior-year/current adoption and 2026 intentions

**Why it matters:** Establishes a current Philippine landscape in which experimentation is widespread while the report argues that scaling beyond proof-of-concept remains difficult. The public page says 175 organizations were surveyed and provides respondent composition and limitations.

**Known limitations:**

- industry-produced, not official national statistics;
- technology/IT services are 37% of the sample;
- 55% of sampled organizations have fewer than 100 employees;
- self-reported survey evidence;
- public page contains a material numerical inconsistency: narrative text says 65% remain at proof-of-concept while the displayed `nature of AI project(s)` chart labels proof-of-concept at 40%;
- exact denominator/question logic behind the 65% figure must be obtained from the methodology appendix or clarified with the researchers before Portfilo uses it as a central statistic.

**Use:** Landscape, source leads, interview target. Do not use the 65% figure without resolving the discrepancy recorded in `contradictions.md`.

## S005 - NPC Advisory No. 2024-04 on AI systems processing personal data

**Source:** National Privacy Commission  
**URL:** https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Advisory-2024.12.19-Guidelines-on-Artificial-Intelligence-w-SGD.pdf  
**Type / tier:** Official regulatory guidance / Tier A  
**Date:** Advisory dated 2024-12-19

**Why it matters:** The advisory applies Data Privacy Act obligations when AI systems process personal data and identifies governance mechanisms including privacy impact assessments, privacy by design/default, security standards, monitoring, human intervention, and documentation.

**Known limitations:** It establishes regulatory expectations, not which controls any specific company actually implemented.

**Use:** Verification framework for governance claims in cases involving personal data.

## Immediate source gaps

The packet still lacks the evidence that matters most for publication:

- direct interviews with operators responsible for moving a system from pilot to production;
- a credible stalled/failed case;
- internal or shareable process documentation below press-release level;
- comparable definitions of `pilot` and `production` across cases;
- operational baselines and measurement methods for claimed benefits;
- independent technical challenge sources who can assess architecture, integration, governance, and failure modes.

These gaps determine whether C001 remains viable as the flagship.
