# C001 Evidence Ledger - AI Pilot to Production

This is a working claim ledger. `VERIFIED` means verified against the cited source for the narrow claim stated here. It does not automatically make the claim central or publishable without context.

**Evidence:** The report page states that 175 Philippine organizations were surveyed and describes respondent composition.

**Source:** S001 - https://www.swarm.work/philippine-ai-report-full

**Source:** S001 - https://www.swarm.work/philippine-ai-report-full  
**Tier:** C  
**Date:** 2025 report  
**Confidence:** LOW until denominator/methodology is reconciled  
**Caveat:** Internal numerical inconsistency or differing denominator/question logic is unresolved.  
**Status:** QUALIFY / UNRESOLVED

**Publication rule:** Do not state `65% remain stuck at POC` as a Portfilo fact until the methodology appendix or Swarm researchers reconcile the 65% narrative with the 40% displayed chart.

## E05

**Candidate claim:** Philippine privacy guidance imposes concrete governance obligations when AI systems process personal data.

**Evidence:** NPC Advisory No. 2024-04 applies Data Privacy Act obligations to development/deployment of AI systems processing personal data and lists governance measures including privacy impact assessment, privacy by design/default, security standards, monitoring, documentation, and meaningful human intervention in relevant automated decisions.

**Source:** S005 - https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Advisory-2024.12.19-Guidelines-on-Artificial-Intelligence-w-SGD.pdf  
**Tier:** A  
**Date:** 2024-12-19  
**Confidence:** HIGH  
**Caveat:** Applicability depends on the processing and facts of each deployment. The advisory does not show whether a specific company complied or which controls it used.  
**Status:** VERIFIED
