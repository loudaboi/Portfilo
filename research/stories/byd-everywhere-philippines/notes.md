# BYD Philippines Research Notes

_Last updated: 2026-09-11_

## Current working answer

Do not treat this as article copy.

The evidence currently suggests that BYD became unusually visible in the Philippines because several conditions lined up:

- the brand introduced plug-in hybrids that reduce dependence on public charging;
- those DM-i models account for most of its volume;
- ACMobility multiplied the dealer network and ownership touchpoints very quickly;
- BYD competes across mainstream price bands rather than only the premium EV market;
- Philippine tariff, tax and traffic-policy incentives reduce electrified-vehicle friction;
- the 2026 fuel-price shock accelerated a broader market shift toward electrified vehicles;
- BYD was simultaneously pushing much harder into overseas markets.

The most important corrective finding is that the Philippine BYD surge is **not primarily a battery-EV story**.

## Useful calculations

Using BYD's January-August 2026 figures:

- DM-i: 22,555 / 28,399 = **79.4%**
- BEV: 5,844 / 28,399 = **20.6%**
- Sealion 6 DM-i: 7,759 / 28,399 = **27.3%**

Dealer network:

- 25 at end-2024
- 79 at end-2025
- 81 in 2026
- end-2024 to end-2025 = **3.16x**, or **216% growth**

Sales:

- 2024: 4,780
- 2025: 26,122
- January-August 2026: 28,399
- 2025 was about **5.46x** 2024 volume
- the first eight months of 2026 were already **8.7% above** full-year 2025

## Open causal questions

### Product

- Is DM-i winning primarily because it eliminates range anxiety?
- How much of the benefit is the ability to run daily trips electrically while keeping long-distance gasoline backup?
- Are Sealion 6, Seal 5 and Atto 2 competing against conventional ICE cars, Japanese hybrids, or other Chinese electrified brands in actual buyer consideration sets?

### Price

- Which genuinely comparable ICE/HEV/PHEV models should be used for price comparison?
- How much of BYD's price position is structurally enabled by zero tariffs and hybrid excise-tax treatment?
- Are transaction prices materially below SRP because of frequent discounts?

### Distribution

- Which of the 81 dealerships are full sales-and-service sites versus pop-ups/showrooms?
- How geographically distributed were the 25 -> 79 openings?
- Did sales growth follow network growth, or did dealer expansion follow already-proven demand?

### Financing

- What share of BYD purchases are financed?
- Are BPI or other bank terms unusually favorable versus conventional competitors?
- How much of the low-down-payment strategy is subsidized by distributor/manufacturer support?

### Fuel shock

- Did BYD's model mix or monthly sales change materially after the early-2026 oil-price shock?
- If monthly model data are unavailable, keep fuel-price effects as an industry-level accelerant rather than a BYD-specific causal claim.

### Trust / ownership

- Does Ayala/ACMobility materially change consumer willingness to buy a relatively new Chinese brand?
- Are parts and repair delays improving as the network scales?
- Do not infer from online discussion. Direct reporting would be needed for a strong buyer-trust claim.

### Global supply

- Did BYD allocate more inventory to the Philippines in 2026?
- Is Philippine pricing affected by global export strategy or is that merely contextual?
- Avoid `dumping`, `overcapacity caused Philippine prices` or geopolitical claims without direct evidence.

## Potential outreach

Not mandatory if the desk evidence supports a narrower explainer, but useful:

### BYD Cars Philippines / ACMobility

Ask for:
- monthly Philippine retail sales by model, January 2024-August 2026;
- current operational-dealer count by type;
- DM-i versus BEV sales explanation;
- average financing share, if disclosable;
- whether 2026 fuel prices changed model mix or demand;
- current parts/after-sales capacity metrics.

### CAMPI / independent automotive analyst

Ask:
- what is driving 2026 PHEV growth;
- whether fuel prices explain the shift or only accelerated an existing trend;
- how to compare BYD with market totals given its non-CAMPI status.

### EVAP / DOE

Ask for:
- current registered BEV/PHEV/HEV fleet or sales if available;
- charging infrastructure definitions;
- policy interpretation relevant to PHEVs.

### Owners / buyers

Only if needed to support motivation:
- why they considered BYD;
- which alternatives they cross-shopped;
- why PHEV versus BEV/HEV/ICE;
- role of price, charging, number coding, dealer access, Ayala backing and fuel cost.

A small set of owner interviews would illustrate decision logic but must not be presented as representative survey evidence.

## Existing-coverage check

Current local coverage already answers:
- how many BYDs have sold;
- which models launched;
- where dealerships opened;
- product-review questions.

Some coverage also offers promotional or opinion-led explanations for why BYD is popular.

The remaining information gap is a disciplined, evidence-aware answer separating:
- product architecture;
- price;
- policy;
- distribution;
- broader xEV demand;
- 2026 fuel conditions;
- global company strategy.
