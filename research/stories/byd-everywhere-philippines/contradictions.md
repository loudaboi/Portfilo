# BYD Philippines Contradiction Search

_Last updated: 2026-09-11_

## Purpose

Challenge the intuitive story before drafting.

## C1 - This is not mainly a full-EV boom

The tempting shorthand is that BYD became ubiquitous because Filipinos suddenly embraced EVs.

But BYD's own January-August 2026 figures show roughly 79% of sales came from DM-i plug-in hybrids.

Implication:
The article should not use `EV boom` as a synonym for BYD's growth without distinguishing BEVs from PHEVs.

## C2 - BYD is not the only brand benefiting from electrification

CAMPI/TMA member data, which excludes BYD, show H1 2026 xEV sales up 132.7% while the overall member market declined 11.4%.

Implication:
Part of BYD's growth reflects a larger market shift. The article needs to explain what is specifically BYD versus what any well-positioned electrified brand could have captured.

## C3 - BYD's early breakout predates the 2026 fuel-price shock

The Sealion 6 DM-i became BYD's top Philippine model in 2024, and BYD's enormous sales jump occurred in 2025 before the 2026 Middle East fuel shock.

Implication:
High 2026 pump prices may accelerate demand, but cannot be the root explanation for the brand's rise.

## C4 - Zero tariffs are an enabler, not a sudden 2026 event

EO 62's zero-tariff treatment for HEVs/PHEVs dates to 2024.

Implication:
Tariff policy helps explain why competitive pricing is possible, but it cannot by itself explain why BYD became dramatically more visible in 2025-2026.

## C5 - The dealership build-out may partly create the perception the question starts from

The network rose from 25 dealerships at end-2024 to 79 at end-2025 and 81 in 2026.

Implication:
`Everywhere` is partly literal retail visibility. The article should distinguish:
- more BYDs on roads;
- more BYD dealerships and mall displays;
- more marketing exposure.

A perception of ubiquity can grow faster than installed vehicle fleet share.

## C6 - Charging infrastructure may not be the decisive BYD advantage

ACMobility has invested heavily in chargers, but most BYDs sold in 2026 are plug-in hybrids that retain a gasoline engine.

Implication:
Do not overstate the charging network as the primary reason buyers chose BYD. It may matter for confidence, brand ecosystem and BEV adoption while being less important to the DM-i majority.

## C7 - Price advantage is not uniform across the lineup

Some BYD entry models are priced far below familiar Japanese hybrids, but the Sealion 6 DM-i at P1.598 million is near the Toyota Corolla Cross G HEV at P1.514 million.

Implication:
The stronger claim may be `BYD offers electrified choices across mainstream price bands`, not `BYD is always cheaper`.

Any peer comparison must match body class, equipment and ownership terms as closely as possible.

## C8 - August financing offers cannot explain the 2024-2025 surge

Current low-down-payment and cash-discount promotions demonstrate aggressive selling but occurred after BYD was already one of the country's fastest-growing brands.

Implication:
Use them as evidence of current strategy, not as retroactive causal explanation.

## C9 - Global Chinese export pressure is context, not a proven local cause

China's domestic vehicle market weakened while BYD's overseas shipments surged in 2026.

Implication:
It is reasonable to say the Philippines is part of a much larger international expansion. It is not yet defensible to say Chinese overcapacity `caused` BYD's Philippine pricing, dealership expansion or customer demand.

## C10 - Company-reported sales are strong evidence of BYD's own volume, but ranking is messy

BYD is not a CAMPI member. Local market tables often mix CAMPI/TMA member data with estimates or separate disclosures from non-members.

Implication:
Avoid an exact current national rank or market-share claim unless the denominator and coverage are clear.

## C11 - Ayala backing may reduce trust friction, but that has not been measured

Community discussions often mention the Ayala/ACMobility relationship as reassuring, while other discussions still report concern about parts, after-sales quality and long-term track record.

Implication:
Do not state `Filipinos trusted BYD because of Ayala` as fact without buyer research. At most, establish that ACMobility supplied a large existing distribution and ownership infrastructure.

## C12 - BYD's own explanation is interested evidence

BYD attributes demand to innovation, practicality, reliability and lower ownership cost.

Implication:
Those are company claims. Reliability and ownership-cost superiority require independent evidence or should be presented only as attributed claims.

## Current adversarial conclusion

The strongest defensible answer is unlikely to be one cause.

The current evidence supports a layered explanation:
- a plug-in-hybrid product that fits incomplete charging conditions;
- rapid distribution expansion;
- competitive/mainstream price positioning;
- favorable policy;
- a broader shift toward electrified vehicles;
- a 2026 fuel-price accelerant;
- a global BYD push to expand overseas.

The relative causal weight of those factors remains open.
