# C021 Source Map

_Last updated: 2026-09-03_

## Source strategy

Prefer current operator pages, company disclosures, BOI/DICT/PCO material, and other first-party records for project status. Use reputable reporting primarily to locate contradictions, capture attributed government statements, or identify projects that require first-party verification.

Do not use paid-market-research headline totals as evidence for the final ledger unless the methodology and metric definition are available.

## Current national baselines

### DICT / BusinessWorld - current and target capacity

BusinessWorld, 25 February 2026:
https://www.bworldonline.com/economy/2026/02/25/732869/data-center-capacity-seen-at-18-gw-within-10-years/

Attributed to DICT Secretary Henry Aguda:

- current Philippine data-center capacity: about `200 MW`;
- possible year-end 2026 capacity: `1 GW`;
- private-sector commitment target: `1.5 GW` by 2028;
- longer-term government ambition: `18 GW` over ten years.

Use only as attributed government baselines. Do not treat targets as operating capacity.

BusinessWorld, 25 March 2026:
https://www.bworldonline.com/technology/2026/03/25/738627/vertiv-says-philippines-on-track-to-reach-1-5-gw-data-center-capacity-target/

Reports current capacity at roughly `200 MW` and the DICT 1.5 GW target.

### DICT / July 2026 reporting

Manila Bulletin, 30 July 2026:
https://mb.com.ph/2026/07/30/pax-silica-ai-hub-seen-more-than-doubling-philippine-data-center-capacity

Reports Aguda saying the Philippines did not yet have 200 MW of current capacity. This timing difference must be reconciled with other 2026 descriptions.

## Operator / project sources

### STT GDC Philippines

Current location index:
https://www.sttelemediagdc.com/ph-en/locations

Current page states seven Philippine data centers and more than 140 MW of IT load, while individual location pages label some facilities as operating, `Launching Soon`, or `Under Development`.

Current detailed examples include:

- STT Cavite 1: `>4 MW` IT load;
- STT Makati: `5 MW` IT load;
- STT Quezon City: `>6 MW` IT load;
- STT Davao: `0.5 MW` IT load;
- STT Fairview 1: `28 MW`, page currently labeled `Launching Soon`;
- STT Fairview 2, 3 and 4: `32 MW` each, labeled `Under Development`;
- STT Cavite 2: `6 MW`, labeled `Launching Soon`.

BusinessWorld, 27 April 2026:
https://www.bworldonline.com/corporate/2026/04/27/745510/stt-gdc-explores-more-phl-sites-for-expansion/

Reports STT GDC Philippines as operating seven data centers with combined IT load of nearly 150 MW, and reports Fairview at a current 28 MW with planned expansion to 32 MW by year-end.

This conflicts with current operator-page labels and is a priority reconciliation item.

Globe, 23 April 2026:
https://www.globe.com.ph/about-us/newsroom/corporate/stt-gdc-ph-mpower-renewable-energy-deal

Reports a 40.5 MW renewable-energy supply agreement for STT Fairview and Cavite campuses. This is power-supply evidence, not by itself IT-load evidence.

### VITRO / ePLDT

VITRO Sta. Rosa current facility page:
https://vitrodc.com/data-centers/vitro-sta-rosa/

States `36 MW IT Load Capacity` and identifies the facility as inaugurated/operating.

BOI, 30 April 2025:
https://boi.gov.ph/boi-official-launch-of-phs-first-ai-ready-hyperscale-data-center-to-bolster-tech-development-in-the-country/

Confirms the April 2025 inauguration of VITRO Sta. Rosa.

VITRO data-center-sites page:
https://vitrodc.com/data-centers/data-center-sites/

Contains older/stale-looking language saying Sta. Rosa would have an initial 10 MW IT power capacity in 2024. Reconcile before treating the full 36 MW as presently live.

### Digital Edge

Current Philippines page:
https://www.digitaledgedc.com/products-services/data-centers/philippines/

NARRA1: `10 MW`, 2,200 cabinets.

Launch record:
https://www.digitaledgedc.com/resources/newsroom/digital-edge-announces-availability-of-its-first-data-center-in-the-philippines/

States NARRA1 was ready for service from 1 March 2023. Strong operating evidence for 10 MW.

### A-FLOW / FLOW / ALLHC

BOI, 4 December 2025:
https://boi.gov.ph/dti-boi-laguna-data-center-project-to-advance-pursuit-for-modern-digital-infrastructure-in-ph/

States ML1's first phase went live with `6 MW` of IT load and the campus can scale to `36 MW`.

FLOW, 28 November 2025:
https://www.flowdigital.com/en/resources/newsroom/a-flow-launches-ml1-philippines%E2%80%99-next-generation-data-center-support-digital-growth-sea

Confirms first phase of `6 MW` within a planned three-building `36 MW` campus.

Treat 6 MW as operating. Do not treat the remaining 30 MW as operating without phase-specific evidence.

### Beeinfotech PH

Current homepage:
https://www.beeinfotech.ph/

States `20 MW+` and more than 3,600 racks for The HIVE.

Grand opening record:
https://www.beeinfotech.ph/post/aidc-driving-the-digital-leap-of-the-philippines-grand-opening-of-beeinfotech-ph-hive-hybrid-data

Confirms March 2025 launch.

The current homepage does not clearly label the 20 MW+ figure as IT load. Keep it out of a strict IT-load subtotal until clarified.

### YCO Cloud

Current site:
https://www.ycocloud.com/

Malvar One states `75 MW` total available utility power and `30 MW energized`.

Current explanatory page:
https://www.ycocloud.com/blog-post/building-the-infrastructure-that-powers-the-world-of-ai-from-the-philippines

Describes Malvar One as a `30 MW powered core and shell`.

Older project record:
https://www.ycocloud.com/blog-post/yco-cloud-breaks-ground-on-rated-iii-data-center-facility

Described the original Malvar One design as `12 MW` critical IT load.

Do not compare 30 MW utility/powered-shell capacity directly with 12 MW IT load. Reconcile current IT-load basis.

### Evolution Data Centres / Megawide

Current Evolution market page:
https://evolutiondatacentres.com/markets-old/

Lists Manila/General Trias at `69 MW IT load`, with RFS `Q3 2026` and Megawide as partner.

Megawide 2023 project announcement:
https://megawide.com.ph/going-digital-megawide-to-build-philippine-data-center-with-singapore-based-evolution-data-centers/

Described 69 MW long-term development and an initial 23 MW phase.

As of 3 September 2026, an RFS target in Q3 is not proof that the facility is operating. Seek a launch/RFS confirmation before moving capacity into `OPERATING`.

### DAMAC Digital

PCO, 13 January 2026:
https://pco.gov.ph/news_releases/president-marcos-meets-damac-digital-execs-to-discuss-data-center-investment-in-ph/

Reports a planned Laguna data-center investment but gives no Philippine project MW.

BOI, June 2026:
https://boi.gov.ph/ph-strengthens-energy-digital-infrastructure-partnerships-with-uae-for-ai-development-under-pax-silica/

Describes progress discussions on a `250 MW` Philippine data-center facility.

Until land, construction, approval or equivalent execution evidence is verified, classify the 250 MW as `ANNOUNCED`, not operating.

### Equinix

Equinix acquisition completion, 3 June 2025:
https://newsroom.equinix.com/2025-06-02-Equinix-Completes-Acquisition-to-Bolster-Digital-Innovation-in-the-Philippines

Confirms three operating Manila data centers, MN1/MN2/MN3, with more than 1,000 cabinets.

Current Philippines page:
https://www.equinix.com/data-centers/asia-pacific-colocation/philippines-colocation

Confirms three Philippine facilities but does not publish a comparable MW figure. Do not estimate MW from cabinets without defensible facility-specific density data.
