# C021 Contradiction Search

_Last updated: 2026-09-03_

## Purpose

Record evidence that could break, narrow, or materially change the working thesis before drafting.

## C1 - DICT current capacity versus identifiable operator numbers

DICT-linked reporting puts Philippine current capacity around or below 200 MW during 2026.

At the same time, April 2026 reporting described STT GDC Philippines alone as operating seven facilities with nearly 150 MW of IT load, before adding Digital Edge, A-FLOW, VITRO, Beeinfotech, Equinix, YCO and other operators.

Possible explanations:

- different definitions of capacity;
- STT portfolio total includes non-operating/future phases;
- government estimate uses a narrower market universe;
- dates do not align;
- some figures refer to design capacity rather than service-ready IT load.

Do not resolve this by choosing the larger or newer number. Reconcile definitions and facility status.

## C2 - STT portfolio total versus facility labels

Current STT location pages distinguish facilities as operating, `Launching Soon`, and `Under Development`, yet the portfolio page states seven data centers and more than 140 MW of IT load. April 2026 reporting described seven operating sites with nearly 150 MW.

Required resolution:

- determine whether Fairview 1 is already operational despite the current page label;
- determine whether portfolio MW includes full Fairview campus development;
- identify exact live MW at each STT site as of the ledger cutoff date.

## C3 - VITRO Sta. Rosa 36 MW versus initial-phase language

The current facility page states 36 MW IT load capacity and the facility was inaugurated in April 2025.

Another VITRO page retains language saying Sta. Rosa would have an initial 10 MW IT power capacity in 2024.

Required resolution:

- establish current service-ready/live IT load;
- determine whether 36 MW is full facility design capacity or fully commissioned capacity.

## C4 - YCO 12 MW IT load versus 30 MW energized/powered shell

YCO's original project release described Malvar One as 12 MW critical IT load. Current material describes 30 MW energized and a 30 MW powered core and shell, with 75 MW utility power available.

These are not necessarily contradictory because they may measure different layers of power infrastructure.

Required resolution:

- obtain current IT-load capacity;
- keep utility power and IT load in separate columns.

## C5 - Evolution RFS Q3 2026 versus no verified launch found

Evolution's current page lists a Q3 2026 RFS for a 69 MW project. As of 3 September 2026, the first pass did not locate a current launch/RFS confirmation.

Required resolution:

- verify whether any phase is actually ready for service;
- if live, determine phase MW rather than automatically counting all 69 MW.

## C6 - National targets are not projects

DICT statements have cited targets or commitments ranging from 1 GW by end-2026 to 1.5 GW by 2028 and 18 GW over ten years.

These numbers must not appear in the same subtotal as operating capacity.

Required treatment:

- present as policy/private-sector targets only;
- map them to identified projects only where evidence supports the connection.

## C7 - DAMAC 250 MW may be more intention than executable capacity

BOI reported progress discussions around a 250 MW facility, while the earlier PCO release described a planned Laguna investment.

Required resolution:

- verify land/site, approval, construction, power, financing or similar execution evidence;
- otherwise retain as `ANNOUNCED`.

## C8 - Important operating facilities may lack public MW

Equinix operates three Manila facilities but publicly describes more than 1,000 cabinets rather than a comparable MW total.

Implication:

A bottom-up national MW total may remain a lower bound if important operating facilities do not publish IT load.

Do not estimate capacity from rack/cabinet counts using generic density assumptions.

## C9 - Third-party installed-capacity figures vary substantially

Current legal/market commentary has cited installed-capacity figures materially above the DICT baseline.

Do not adopt those totals until their methodology, facility universe and power metric are transparent enough to audit.

## Decision rule

If these contradictions can be materially reconciled, continue toward a verified status ledger and national subtotal.

If they cannot, the stronger publishable story may become:

> **Why Philippine data-center capacity numbers conflict — and what operating, energized, planned and announced megawatts actually mean.**

That would be a legitimate reframe, not a failure of C021.
