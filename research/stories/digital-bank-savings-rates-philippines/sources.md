# Philippine Digital Bank Savings Rates Source Inventory

_Last updated: 2026-09-11_

## Tier A - Primary / official

### Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas - Circular No. 1105
URL: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Regulations/Issuances/2020/c1105.pdf

Date: 2020

Why it matters:
Defines the formal Philippine digital-bank classification and its branchless/end-to-end digital operating model.

Limitations:
Regulatory design does not prove the current cost structure or profitability of the sector.

### BSP - Selected Performance Indicators, Digital Bank Group
URL: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Statistics/Selected%20Performance%20Indicators/94_data.aspx

Data used: June 2026, page updated July 24, 2026

Why it matters:
Primary group-level evidence for deposits, loans, funding cost, earning-asset yield, net interest margin, cost-to-income and profitability.

Key June 2026 values:
- deposits: P171.545 billion
- gross loans: P82.462 billion
- loans-to-deposits: 48.07%
- earning-asset yield: 16.97%
- funding cost: 4.15%
- net interest margin: 12.84%
- cost-to-income ratio: 75.39%
- annualized net profit/loss: negative P5.252 billion

Limitations:
Group aggregate. Does not show the economics of each individual digital bank.

### BSP - Selected Performance Indicators, Universal and Commercial Bank Group
URL: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Statistics/Selected%20Performance%20Indicators/16_data.aspx

Data used: June 2026, page updated July 24, 2026

Why it matters:
Reference point for the economics of large incumbent banks.

Key June 2026 values:
- earning-asset yield: 5.94%
- funding cost: 1.87%
- net interest margin: 4.28%
- cost-to-income ratio: 54.02%
- annualized ROA: 1.41%

Limitations:
A group comparison does not isolate savings-account pricing and the two bank groups have different asset mixes, scale and business models.

### BSP - Deposit Liabilities by Type and Size of Accounts
URL: https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Statistics/Deposit%20Accounts/72_data.aspx

Data used: March 2026 table and later BSP releases

Why it matters:
Confirms the large number of digital-bank deposit accounts and group deposit base.

Limitations:
Account counts are not unique customers and deposit-size distributions should not be interpreted as household-level behavior without care.

### Maya Bank - High-interest savings
URL: https://www.mayabank.ph/highinterestsavings/

Accessed: 2026-09-11

Why it matters:
Current first-party terms show a base savings rate of 3% p.a. and a headline rate up to 15% p.a. under promotional conditions.

Limitations:
The 15% rate is not a universal base rate. Current promotion terms apply conditions and a P100,000 balance cap.

### Maya - September 2026 high-interest promotion
URL: https://www.mayabank.ph/highinterestsavings/

Accessed: 2026-09-11

Why it matters:
Shows how a large headline rate can operate as a targeted customer-acquisition/engagement promotion rather than a permanent all-balance rate.

Current mechanics shown:
- September 1-30, 2026 promotion;
- new Maya users must sign up, upgrade and deposit at least P1,000;
- 15% p.a. consists of 3% base plus 12% boosted interest;
- bonus applies to up to P100,000;
- qualification lasts for one calendar month.

### UNO Digital Bank - #UNOready Savings
URL: https://www.uno.bank/savings-account/

Accessed: 2026-09-11

Why it matters:
Current first-party BSP-licensed digital-bank savings pricing.

Current rates:
- 3.00% p.a. below P5,000;
- 3.50% p.a. from P5,000 to P4,999,999.99;

### Tonik Digital Bank - Deposit Interest Rates

### Philippine Deposit Insurance Corporation - Maximum Deposit Insurance Coverage
URL: https://www-origin.pdic.gov.ph/mdic

Effective date: 2025-03-15

Why it matters:
Deposits in member banks are insured up to P1 million per depositor, per bank.

Limitations:
Deposit insurance does not mean all financial risks, fraud risks or operational risks disappear.

## Tier B - Strong secondary / institutional context

### The Philippine Star - Fitch on Philippine digital-bank deposit pricing
URL: https://www.philstar.com/business/2023/12/05/2316427/price-focused-strategy-philippines-digital-banks-not-sustainable-fitch

Date: 2023-12-05

Why it matters:
Reports Fitch's view that Philippine digital banks were competing aggressively for deposits to build scale, often using price because their franchises were still nascent, and that a price-only strategy would be difficult to sustain.

Limitations:
The analysis predates current 2026 sector data and should be used as strategy context, not as proof of every bank's current motive.

### Philippine Information Agency - BSP digital-bank adoption briefing
URL: https://pia.gov.ph/regions/philippines-ready-for-digital-adoption-says-bangko-sentral/

Why it matters:
Records the standard policy explanation that branchless operations can lower some operational costs and support higher deposit rates.

Limitations:
This is a high-level explanation. Current BSP performance data show that digital banks still have high aggregate operating costs relative to income, so it should not be treated as the full answer.

### Congressional Policy and Budget Research Department - virtual banking policy brief
URL: https://cpbrd.congress.gov.ph/pb2020-14-strengthening-virtual-banking-regulation-to-promote-financial-inclusion-in-the-philippines/

Date: 2020

Why it matters:
Provides early Philippine policy rationale for branchless banking, lower physical-network costs and more competitive deposit products.

Limitations:
Predates the operating history of today's licensed digital-bank group.
