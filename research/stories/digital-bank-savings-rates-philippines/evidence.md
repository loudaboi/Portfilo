# Philippine Digital Bank Savings Rates Evidence Ledger

_Last updated: 2026-09-11_

## C01 - The savings-rate gap is real and currently large

Candidate claim:
Ordinary savings products at major incumbent banks currently pay far less than several BSP-licensed digital-bank savings products.

Evidence:
- BDO ATM Savings: 0.0625% gross p.a.
- BPI Regular Savings: 0.0625% p.a.
- Maya Savings: 3% base p.a.
- UNO #UNOready: 3.00%-3.50% p.a. across most balances below P5 million.
- Tonik Solo Stash: 4.00% p.a.

Sources:
BDO, BPI, Maya Bank, UNO Digital Bank, Tonik Digital Bank first-party product pages.

Tier:
A

Confidence:
HIGH

Caveat:
Products have different conditions, balance thresholds and features. Do not imply every digital product always beats every traditional-bank product.

Status:
VERIFIED

## C02 - A 3% base rate is about 48 times a 0.0625% ordinary savings rate

Candidate claim:
The gap can be orders of magnitude even before promotional boosts.

Calculation:
3.00 / 0.0625 = 48

Source:
Maya base savings rate; BDO/BPI ordinary savings rate.

Tier:
A + calculation

Confidence:
HIGH

Caveat:
This is a rate comparison, not a comparison of total account value, service quality, liquidity features or risk.

Status:
VERIFIED

## C03 - Digital banks pay materially more for funding at the group level

Candidate claim:
Higher savings rates show up in the sector's actual funding economics, not only in marketing copy.

Evidence:
BSP June 2026:
- Digital Bank Group funding cost: 4.15%
- Universal and Commercial Bank Group funding cost: 1.87%

Calculation:
4.15 / 1.87 = about 2.22x.

Source:
BSP Selected Performance Indicators.

Tier:
A

Confidence:
HIGH

Caveat:
Funding cost includes interest-bearing liabilities beyond a single savings product. It is not the average advertised savings rate.

Status:
VERIFIED

## C04 - Digital banks also earn much higher yields on earning assets

Candidate claim:
The digital-bank group has more room to pay for funding because its earning assets produce much higher yields than the large-bank group.

Evidence:
BSP June 2026:
- Digital Bank Group earning-asset yield: 16.97%
- Universal and Commercial Bank Group: 5.94%

Calculation:
16.97 / 5.94 = about 2.86x.

Source:
BSP Selected Performance Indicators.

Tier:
A

Confidence:
HIGH for the comparison.

Caveat:
The group-level yield difference does not establish exactly which assets fund any specific deposit product. It also reflects different asset mixes and risk profiles.

Status:
VERIFIED / QUALIFY CAUSAL LANGUAGE

## C05 - The digital-bank group's net interest margin is also much higher

Candidate claim:
Even after funding cost, digital banks as a group generate a much wider interest margin than universal/commercial banks.

Evidence:
BSP June 2026:
- Digital Bank Group NIM: 12.84%
- Universal and Commercial Bank Group NIM: 4.28%

Calculation:
12.84 / 4.28 = exactly 3.0x.

Source:
BSP Selected Performance Indicators.

Tier:
A

Confidence:
HIGH

Caveat:
A high NIM does not mean the sector is highly profitable after credit costs and operating expenses.

Status:
VERIFIED

## C06 - "No branches means lower costs" is incomplete

Candidate claim:
Branchless operations remove a category of physical-network cost, but current digital banks are not cheaper overall relative to income.

Evidence:
BSP June 2026 cost-to-income ratios:
- Digital Bank Group: 75.39%
- Universal and Commercial Bank Group: 54.02%

The BSP regulatory definition also confirms that licensed digital banks do not operate physical branches/sub-branches or branch-lite units offering financial products and services.

Source:
BSP Circular No. 1105; BSP Selected Performance Indicators.

Tier:
A

Confidence:
HIGH

Caveat:
Cost-to-income is an efficiency ratio, not a direct measure of branch expense. It shows that current aggregate operating economics are more complicated than a simple lower-overhead story.

Status:
VERIFIED

## C07 - The digital-bank group is still loss-making in aggregate

Candidate claim:
Higher deposit rates are not simply surplus profit being handed back to savers.

Evidence:
BSP June 2026 selected indicators report annualized net profit/loss for the Digital Bank Group at negative P5.252 billion and annualized ROA of negative 3.14%.

Source:
BSP Selected Performance Indicators, Digital Bank Group.

Tier:
A

Confidence:
HIGH

Caveat:
The group aggregate can remain negative even when individual banks are profitable.

Status:
VERIFIED

## C08 - Deposits materially exceed loans in the digital-bank group

Candidate claim:
Digital banks are not simply taking one peso of deposits and immediately turning it into one peso of loans.

Evidence:
BSP June 2026:
- deposits: P171.545 billion
- gross loans: P82.462 billion
- loans-to-deposits ratio: 48.07%
- liquid assets to deposits: 61.94%

Source:
BSP Selected Performance Indicators.

Tier:
A

Confidence:
HIGH

Caveat:
Banks also hold securities, cash and other earning/liquid assets. This does not show how a particular bank allocates a particular depositor's money.

Status:
VERIFIED

## C09 - The highest advertised digital-bank rates can be conditional and capped

Candidate claim:
A headline such as "up to 15%" should not be read as the normal rate applied to every peso in every account.

Evidence:
Maya's September 2026 promotion:
- 3% base rate;
- 12% boosted interest for qualifying new users;
- qualification requires account actions and at least P1,000 deposited;
- bonus applies only up to P100,000;
- promotion applies for one calendar month after qualification.

UNO's current bonus campaign similarly applies extra interest only to qualifying fresh funds, for selected customers and subject to a P100,000 qualifying amount cap.

Sources:
Maya and UNO first-party promotion terms.

Tier:
A

Confidence:
HIGH

Caveat:
Promo mechanics change frequently. Any final article must recheck them immediately before publication.

Status:
VERIFIED / TIME-SENSITIVE

## C10 - Aggressive deposit pricing has been used as a growth strategy

Candidate claim:
For newer digital-bank franchises, higher deposit rates can serve as a way to attract funding and customers while the bank builds scale.

Evidence:
Fitch, as reported by The Philippine Star in December 2023, said Philippine digital banks were expected to compete aggressively for deposits while refining business models and building scale, with many relying on price to attract customers because their franchises were nascent.

Source:
The Philippine Star report on Fitch analysis.

Tier:
B

Confidence:
MEDIUM-HIGH

Caveat:
This is sector strategy analysis from 2023. It should support the mechanism, not be presented as a current motive of every bank in 2026.

Status:
QUALIFY

## C11 - Deposits are insured up to P1 million per depositor, per bank

Candidate claim:
A high savings rate does not by itself mean the deposit sits outside the normal Philippine deposit-insurance framework.

Evidence:
PDIC raised maximum deposit insurance coverage from P500,000 to P1 million per depositor, per bank, effective March 15, 2025.

Source:
Philippine Deposit Insurance Corporation.

Tier:
A

Confidence:
HIGH

Caveat:
Deposit insurance is not a blanket guarantee against every type of loss or service failure.

Status:
VERIFIED

## C12 - "Digital bank" is a formal regulatory class in the Philippines

Candidate claim:
Not every bank with an app or app-only consumer experience is a BSP-classified digital bank.

Evidence:
BSP Circular No. 1105 created Digital Banks as a distinct bank classification. BSP's 2026 payments material lists six authorized digital banks: GoTyme Bank, Maya Bank, Overseas Filipino Bank, Tonik Digital Bank, UnionDigital Bank and UNObank.

Source:
BSP Circular No. 1105; BSP Payments Bulletin / banking statistics.

Tier:
A

Confidence:
HIGH

Caveat:
Consumer-facing articles and comparison sites often use "digital bank" more loosely. The Portfilo article should define which meaning it uses.

Status:
VERIFIED
