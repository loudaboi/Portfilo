# Portfilo Homepage Hero Copy v3

_Status: FOUNDER APPROVED / HERO ONLY / NO RUNTIME IMPLEMENTATION AUTHORIZED_

_Date: 2026-09-08_

## Decision

The founder approved a new homepage newsletter hero direction after rejecting the prior `briefing`-led candidate and the weaker category-description attempts.

This decision supersedes the hero wording in `research/homepage-copy-v2.md`.

The approved acquisition pattern is intentionally closer to strong newsletter marketing such as BackScoop and Morning Brew: a confident reader-facing command/outcome first, followed by a concrete explanation of what arrives and the operating promise.

No wording is copied from those publications.

## Exact approved hero copy

Label:

`NEWSLETTER`

Headline:

> **Get ahead in business and tech in 7 minutes.**

Supporting sentence:

> **The latest news on companies, startups, products, markets, AI, and technology. Three times a week, straight to your inbox.**

Form behavior remains:

- email input;
- `Subscribe free` action;
- double-opt-in confirmation;
- unsubscribe support.

## Founder-approved product promises created by this copy

This approval intentionally creates two public operating commitments that were previously unapproved:

1. **reading-time promise:** an issue is positioned as a 7-minute read;
2. **cadence promise:** the newsletter is delivered three times per week.

It also intentionally authorizes the phrase **latest news** as the public acquisition promise for the newsletter.

These are no longer hypothetical copy claims. Editorial planning, issue construction, and newsletter operations must be capable of meeting them before public launch.

Exact send days/times are **not** approved by this decision.

## Public-language correction

`Briefing` may remain inside historical/internal product-model research where it describes an archetype, but it is **rejected as public homepage/newsletter acquisition language** at this boundary unless the founder explicitly reopens it.

Likewise, do not replace the approved concrete scope with newsroom-format language such as `reporting`, `Features`, `deeper stories`, or `current stories` merely to make the copy sound editorial.

## Scope represented by the support line

The approved support line names the concrete coverage areas the newsletter can contain:

- companies;
- startups;
- products;
- markets;
- AI;
- technology.

This is reader-facing acquisition copy, not a replacement for the site's separate navigation/taxonomy model.

## Section-specific signup rule

The founder explicitly decided that newsletter signup copy should **not be duplicated across homepage sections**.

The hero copy above is approved only for the hero.

The later/final homepage signup must be written for the context immediately preceding it rather than repeating this hero headline or support line. That lower-page signup remains an open copy gate.

## Superseded copy

`research/homepage-copy-v2.md` is now rejected for current hero use. In particular, do not restore:
