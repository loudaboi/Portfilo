## Typography under test

### Display

**Bricolage Grotesque**

Reason to test:

- variable optical-size, width, and weight axes;
- strong enough to carry the visual system without imitating the Noto Serif Display wordmark;
- character comes from drawing/proportion rather than decorative effects;
- open-source under SIL OFL 1.1.

This is a specimen candidate, not approved production typography.

### Interface / support

**Onest**

Reason to test:

- designed for screen/UI reading;
- restrained geometric-humanist character;
- distinct from both Bricolage Grotesque and the Portfilo wordmark;
- open-source under SIL OFL 1.1.

This is a specimen candidate, not approved production typography.

## Composition hypothesis

The hero is treated as a measured typesetting surface.

The headline is split into three stepped measures:

1. `Software built`
2. `around the`
3. `problem.`

The lines do not behave as a conventional left-aligned hero text box.

Different display-width settings are used deliberately to test whether the proposition can become the primary visual event.

The approved support sentence is removed from the conventional position directly under the headline.

Instead, it becomes a lower-right information rail at readable contrast.

Primary navigation occupies the lower-left rail.

The emblem and wordmark remain calm perimeter anchors.
