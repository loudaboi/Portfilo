# Portfilo v1 Hero Visual Material Gate

_Last updated: 2026-09-15_

## Status

**FOUNDER-APPROVED EXPLORATION / ACTIVE**

## Decision job

Find the visual event that completes the selected dark Perimeter Field hero without reopening its structure.

## Fixed hero foundation

- dark-first near-black environment;
- Sharp Ornamental 01 emblem upper-left;
- approved PORTFILO wordmark upper-right;
- Mona Sans website typography;
- supporting copy upper-left;
- proposition low-left;
- vertical navigation on the right perimeter.

The layout itself is not the decision being tested.

## Candidate A — Responsive geometric field

A living abstract system made from controlled boundaries, planes, apertures, cuts, masks, and spatial transitions.

Intent:

**make the identity feel responsive without turning the emblem itself into a gimmick.**

Reject if it resembles generic motion-design wallpaper, HUD graphics, or decorative line art.

## Candidate B — Sculptural graphic object

One strong graphic form with real visual gravity.

Intent:

**give Portfilo a memorable hero image without relying on photography or fake product imagery.**

Reject if it becomes a generic chrome object, luxury 3D render, random sculpture, or art direction unrelated to software/problem-solving.

## Candidate C — System-resolution visual

An abstract visual system where fragments, constraints, or discontinuous elements resolve into coherent structure.

Intent:

**connect visually to software built around a problem without becoming an explainer diagram.**

Reject if it becomes literal process illustration, flowchart UI, or generic AI network imagery.

## Shared generation contract

Generated assets must contain:

- no text;
- no Portfilo logo;
- no wordmark;
- no navigation;
- no buttons;
- no devices;
- no UI;
- no fake Work;
- no fake people;
- no stock photography.

Avoid:

- chrome spheres;
- aurora gradients;
- random particles;
- neon AI visual language;
- generic 3D blobs;
- glassmorphism;
- decorative noise with no structural role.

## Review rule

Judge the visual material independently first.

Then composite only the selected candidate into the exact hero.

Do not let image generation decide copy, layout, typography, navigation, or identity placement.
