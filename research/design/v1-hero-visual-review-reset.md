# Portfilo v1 Hero Visual Review Reset

_Last updated: 2026-09-15_

## Status

**FOUNDER REVIEW COMPLETE / VISUAL EXPLORATION REOPENED**

## What passed

The founder still likes:

- Sharp Ornamental 01 emblem;
- approved PORTFILO wordmark;
- the Assembly loader-to-resolved-hero behavior as an interaction idea.

These remain active evidence.

## What failed

The latest hero material study did not achieve the desired visual quality.

Rejected as current visual direction:

- Crossfield;
- Refraction;
- Strata.

Observed problems:

- the full-screen background still did not feel like the kind of beautiful hero environment the founder had in mind;
- the current website font treatment no longer feels right;
- the muted supporting paragraph feels visually misplaced / disconnected from the composition;
- the total hero still feels weaker than the approved emblem + wordmark.

## Important interpretation

Do not solve this by tweaking shader parameters or spacing.

The problem is broader:

**the visual hierarchy and typographic composition around the identity need to be re-explored together with the hero background.**

The approved identity assets should be treated as the strongest current anchor.

## Typography

Mona Sans is reopened for review.

This does not automatically mean Mona Sans is permanently rejected across the whole site.

It means:

- do not treat it as locked hero display typography;
- re-evaluate display type, body/support type, scale, weight, width, and contrast;
- consider whether one family should handle the whole interface or whether a restrained secondary family is justified.

The Noto Serif Display wordmark remains untouched.

## Supporting paragraph

The approved support copy itself is not rewritten by this review.

What is reopened is its **presentation**:

- placement;
- width;
- contrast;
- typographic treatment;
- whether it belongs visibly in the first viewport at all.

Do not invent replacement copy without a separate copy decision.

## Assembly

Assembly motion remains approved as an interaction concept.

However:

- Assembly is not allowed to dictate the visual design;
- the next exploration may simplify or reposition the hero composition around the visual;
- if a stronger visual direction eventually makes Assembly unnecessary, that should be raised explicitly rather than silently preserving it.

## Exact next gate

**HERO VISUAL + TYPOGRAPHIC RE-EXPLORATION**

In the next session:

1. inspect the current repo authority;
2. treat emblem + wordmark as the strongest visual anchors;
3. reopen hero typography;
4. reopen support-copy composition;
5. reject Crossfield / Refraction / Strata;
6. research stronger examples of full-screen hero art direction and typography as one system;
7. do not jump straight into another shader or mockup;
8. propose a small number of materially different visual/typographic directions;
9. only prototype after founder selection.

No merge to main or deployment is authorized.
