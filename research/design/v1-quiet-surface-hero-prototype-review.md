## Rendered critique

### Specificity

The static frame remains deliberately restrained and would not carry the entire Portfilo identity by itself.

That is consistent with the selected direction: the strongest authorship is reserved for state change, continuity, and interaction.

The static frame now carries Portfilo through:

- the approved emblem/wordmark relationship;
- the exact problem-led proposition;
- the studio/editorial tension between the wordmark and website grotesk;
- the selected quiet surface.

Do not add decorative material merely to increase static distinctiveness.

### Hierarchy

Pass after correction.

Order:

1. headline;
2. identity / primary navigation;
3. support explanation.

The headline was reduced from the first pass so it no longer dominates the entire viewport.

### Coherence

Pass.

The serif wordmark provides the refined brand voice while the grotesk carries software/interface communication.

The grainy dark surface remains supporting material rather than a competing visual object.

### Restraint

Pass.

No:

- abstract hero object;
- filler imagery;
- fake Work;
- extra labels;
- arbitrary rules;
- detached information rail;
- decorative center composition.

### Craft corrections made before founder review

First render exposed:

- headline too close to the previous oversized-display failure;
- unreliable header geometry in the review render;
- identity contrast needed to be explicit;
- full five-item navigation needed reliable right alignment.

Correction pass:

- headline reduced to 84 px reference territory;
- hero pinned to a deliberate lower-left resting position;
- approved identity geometry forced to off-white in the prototype;
- header geometry made explicit;
- support measure retained directly beneath headline;
- navigation keeps all five approved labels.

## Founder rejection outcome

This static prototype is rejected.

Observed failures:

- unapproved blue tint entered the surface;
- grain treatment was too weak to read as the intended material;
- composition regressed to a generic creative-tech / agency hero;
- left-heavy large sans headline + empty right field repeated the same failure pattern the redesign was supposed to eliminate;
- motion, transitions, and pointer behavior were incorrectly deferred as decoration rather than treated as part of the hero's defining behavior;
- the rendered result should have been rejected internally by the visual-direction skill before founder review.

Do not tune this prototype.

Do not preserve its composition, color treatment, or hierarchy under new cosmetics.

Retained evidence only:

- approved identity assets;
- approved copy;
- the founder's preference for a grainy neutral dark resting surface;
- motion / transitions / pointer behavior as core design material.

The next hero proposal must be materially different at the composition level.


## Additional founder correction — identity composition

The prototype also failed by joining the Sharp Ornamental 01 emblem and PORTFILO wordmark into a conventional horizontal lockup in the hero/header.

That treatment is founder-rejected for the homepage.

Do not use the combined horizontal lockup as the hero identity treatment.

Retain both approved assets independently:

- Sharp Ornamental 01 emblem;
- PORTFILO wordmark.

Their relationship should be created through composition, placement, scale, state, and motion rather than physically joining them into one logo unit.
