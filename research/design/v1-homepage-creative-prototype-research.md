# Portfilo Homepage Creative Prototype Research

_Last updated: 2026-09-14_

## Purpose

Support the homepage creative-prototype gate after rejection of the Obsidian Precision implementation.

## Research takeaways

### Identity should drive the digital experience

ET Studio's Nine To Five project describes identity as the thread running through the entire digital experience, with modularity designed to interact with navigation and the site itself.

Reference:
https://www.e-t.studio/works/nine-to-five-studio

Portfilo implication:

The approved Sharp Ornamental 01 identity should inform composition and interaction, not simply sit in the header.

### Motion should belong to the identity

Studio Dumbar's work for Instagram focuses on a motion system with purpose and rhythm rather than isolated transitions.

Reference:
https://studiodumbar.com/work/instagram

Portfilo implication:

The homepage should have one consistent motion behavior that clarifies state or composition instead of repeated reveal animations.

### Navigation can become part of the identity

Studio K95 turns portfolio navigation into the central spatial experience.

Reference:
https://www.cssdesignawards.com/sites/studio-k95/49604/

Portfilo implication:

The memorable behavior may come from how users move between Portfilo surfaces rather than from a decorative hero effect.

### Strong digital experiences can remain minimal

ET Studio's Nine To Five work explicitly positions boldness and minimalism together, while Sileent uses black-and-white identity, simultaneous moving media, and navigation behavior as part of the brand.

References:
https://www.e-t.studio/works/nine-to-five-studio
https://www.e-t.studio/works/sileent

Portfilo implication:

Minimal does not need to mean static, empty, or conventional.

### Software language can become visual material when authentic

Studio Dumbar's OpenAI DevDay 2025 identity derives layout and motion from coding conventions such as indentation, functions, glyphs, input/output, and light/dark states.

Reference:
https://studiodumbar.com/work/openai-devday-2025

Portfilo implication:

Software-native behavior should come from meaningful state, hierarchy, and transformation, not terminal styling or fake code motifs.

## Rejected lesson from the previous homepage

The old homepage had structure but no memorable behavior.

It relied on:

- large static headline;
- black field;
- muted hierarchy;
- ruled section stacking;
- generic reveal motion.

The new creative prototype must therefore be evaluated first on:

1. signature behavior;
2. atmosphere;
3. identity integration;
4. content scalability;
5. restraint.

A layout that is merely clean should not pass.
