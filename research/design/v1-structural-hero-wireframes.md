## Shared constraints

All three wireframes use:

- 1440 × 900 reference viewport;
- founder-approved Mona Sans website typography;
- approved Portfilo wordmark and Sharp Ornamental 01 emblem;
- exact approved hero headline;
- exact approved supporting sentence;
- exact Work / Services / Hustle / Journal / Contact navigation labels;
- grayscale only;
- no imagery;
- no invented CTA;
- no fake Work;
- no final color, material, motion, or polish.

These are composition studies only.

## A — Perimeter Field

Core idea:

**The interface lives on the perimeter while the proposition owns the center/lower field.**

Structure:

- emblem and wordmark separated across the top perimeter;
- navigation runs vertically on the right edge;
- supporting copy occupies a compact upper-left position;
- headline anchors the lower-left / lower-center;
- one deliberately empty spatial field is reserved for a future meaningful interaction.

Strength:

- least conventional marketing-header behavior;
- strong use of the full viewport;
- can become expressive without requiring fake media.

Risk:

- perimeter navigation could feel art-directed rather than software-native if the later interaction does not earn it.

## B — Identity Rail

Core idea:

**A persistent identity/navigation rail leaves the rest of the viewport free to behave as a software surface.**

Structure:

- wordmark, navigation, and emblem live in a permanent left rail;
- headline occupies the main upper field;
- supporting copy anchors the lower field;
- one compact state/interaction area remains intentionally unresolved.

Strength:

- clearest route from static composition to stateful Responsive Identity behavior;
- strongest software-system logic;
- navigation remains easy to understand.

Risk:

- can become dashboard/sidebar-like if treated too literally.

## C — Cross Axis

Core idea:

**Navigation becomes the structural horizon separating proposition from responsive identity/content space.**

Structure:

- emblem at one upper edge;
- wordmark centered independently;
- headline occupies the upper field;
- navigation forms a horizontal axis across the viewport;
- supporting copy and future identity state occupy opposite lower zones.

Strength:

- strong hierarchy without a conventional header;
- balanced between editorial and software behavior.

Risk:

- could become overly diagrammatic if the horizontal axis is treated as decoration rather than functional structure.

## Founder selection

**A — Perimeter Field**

The founder selected Perimeter Field as the homepage structural direction.

This selection locks the structural logic only:

- identity separated across the top perimeter;
- vertical navigation on the right edge;
- compact supporting copy upper-left;
- headline anchored lower-left / lower-center;
- intentional open spatial field.

It does not lock the full homepage, motion system, media, or below-fold composition.
