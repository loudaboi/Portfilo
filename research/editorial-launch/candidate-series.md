# Portfilo Editorial Launch Research - Series Check

_Last updated: 2026-09-02_

## Purpose

Test whether the Stage 6 passed candidates should launch as a formal series before the final launch-package recommendation is made.

A series is justified only when multiple independently strong stories answer distinct parts of one larger investigation and the reader gains something material from following them together.

Shared subject matter is not enough.

## Candidate clusters tested

### AI implementation cluster

Candidates:

- C001 - AI pilot to production;
- C002 - all-in cost of one AI customer-support workflow;
- C007 - buy vs integrate vs build for a defined AI workflow.

Assessment:

The questions are related, but they are not yet a proven series.

C001 is the strongest independent reported question. C002 may become a strong standalone decision story only if reporting yields an auditable cost base. C007 is useful but narrower and could become a follow-up or derivative if the same reporting produces enough comparative evidence.

Recommendation:

Do not brand these as a launch series. Treat C002 and C007 as possible follow-ons unlocked by C001 research.

### IT-BPM transition cluster

Candidates:

- C009 - what "higher-value work" actually means;
- C011 - AI-related skill requirements in job postings.

Assessment:

C011 is currently stronger as a dataset or reporting module inside C009 than as a required second installment.

Recommendation:

Do not create a two-story series. Lead with C009. Spin out C011 only if the job-posting analysis produces a distinct independent finding.

### Payments cluster

Candidates:

- C017 - who ultimately pays for zero-fee small-merchant payments;
- C018 - what Direct Debit PH changes for recurring-payment businesses;
- C020 - Direct Debit PH vs cards vs transfer-based collection.

Assessment:

The three questions share payment infrastructure but serve different decisions. Publishing all three together at launch would overconcentrate the package and create avoidable research duplication.

Recommendation:

Do not launch them as a series. C017 is the strongest independent launch candidate. C018 and C020 should remain possible follow-ons if source access and real business-use evidence justify them.

### Data-center cluster

Candidates:

- C021 - verified capacity status;
- C023 - what makes a data center genuinely AI-ready.

Assessment:

C021 has a clear verification contribution. C023 is useful but may become vendor-defined unless independent technical criteria are established.

Recommendation:

Do not create a two-story series. C023 can become a sidebar, explainer, or later standalone story if the technical evidence becomes strong enough.

### Startup financing

Candidate:

- C026 - financing structures beyond traditional equity.

Assessment:

There is no second passed startup candidate strong enough to justify a series at launch.

## Conclusion

**No formal launch series is justified.**

The strongest Stage 6 candidates form several useful reporting clusters, but the dependencies and overlaps are better handled through shared research, follow-on stories, and derivative formats rather than a branded series.

Portfilo should launch with a **coherent editorial package of independent stories**, not a formal series.

This keeps the launch broad enough to demonstrate the publication's range while preserving the option to create a future series only when reporting reveals real depth.
