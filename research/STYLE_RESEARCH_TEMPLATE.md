## Story purpose

## Audience assumptions

What can the intended reader reasonably be expected to know already?

Which terms, mechanisms, or context require explanation?

Which audience-interest assumptions are:

- established by Portfilo's approved audience/product definition;
- supported by external audience research;
- editorial hypotheses that should not be presented as fact?

Examples:

- current development;
- chronology;
- dataset;
- system/mechanism;
- reported scene;
- interview material;
- comparison;
- argument plus counterargument;
- practical decision;
- technical/regulatory detail.

## Cross-example findings

Separate observation from editorial decision.

### Observed across references

What practices recur across more than one relevant source/example?

What materially differs between them?

### Portfilo interpretation

Which recurring practices fit this assignment, and why?

Which practices should Portfilo reject for this assignment, and why?

Do not choose a practice merely because it appears frequently.
