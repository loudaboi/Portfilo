## Why this is only a pilot-selection approval

This approval authorizes visual concept research for C030.

It does **not** authorize:

- an image-generation prompt;
- an `image_gen` call;
- site/schema/image-component implementation;
- selecting an image without human review;
- publishing any generated visual.

The canonical authority remains:

- `docs/IMAGE_SYSTEM.md`;
- `research/IMAGE_BRIEF_TEMPLATE.md`;
- `AGENTS.md`.

## Exact next task

1. Inspect C030's approved body, final review, style research/brief, and any evidence/provenance boundaries relevant to imagery.
2. Perform the image-necessity gate.
3. Extract the visual problem from the approved editorial material.
4. Develop **2-3 genuinely different visual concepts**.
5. For each concept, record:
   - visual subject;
   - why it represents the story;
   - what it deliberately leaves out;
   - factual or documentary-misreading risk;
   - expected 16:9 / 1200x630 / 4:3 / 1:1 crop behavior;
   - whether Portfilo blue, Portfilo red, or no chromatic accent is justified.
6. Present those concepts to Louda Tuppal for selection.
7. Only after human concept selection, create `research/stories/what-actually-changed/image-brief.md`.
8. Obtain explicit approval of that image brief before any image generation.
