## Image job

Create one publication-level homepage background image whose primary job is atmosphere and visual identity.

The image does **not** explain the article topic.

The existing homepage HTML remains the information layer.

## Scene lock

Use one compact contemporary office or creative studio viewed from a slightly elevated corner angle.

The scene must contain exactly:

- two simple worktables;
- one low shelving unit;
- one plain structural partition;
- exactly two anonymous human figures;
- clear walking space between the furniture.

No additional meaningful furniture or narrative objects are authorized.

## Human-presence lock

Exactly two people:

1. one seated at a desk in the deeper right side of the room, visible only as a dark indistinct silhouette;
2. one walking through the middle-right passage, visibly motion-blurred.

Controls:

- no readable face;
- no portrait emphasis;
- no expressive gesture;
- no third person;
- neither person may become the focal subject.

## Composition lock

The left half and central-upper region must remain predominantly near-black and low-detail for the existing light-blue HTML overlay.

Concentrate the brighter working area, furniture detail, and human activity toward the right half.

The room should feel compact and intimate rather than monumental.

Furniture placement and circulation must be immediately understandable.

## Lighting and tonal treatment

- black and white / grayscale only;
- predominantly dark overall;
- one restrained practical light source or one simple localized working-light zone on the right;
- deep blacks;
- restrained highlights;
- no bright ceiling-wide illumination;
- no cinematic spectacle.

## Grain rule

Visible grain is required.

Target:

`MEDIUM-VISIBLE EDITORIAL GRAIN`

The grain must be clearly noticeable across dark and midtone areas without obscuring structure, human silhouettes, or HTML-overlay readability.

Avoid clean, glossy, plastic-smooth rendering.

## Physical/logical plausibility

All scene relationships must make ordinary real-world sense.

Check before approval:

- tables stand normally and do not intersect walls or people;
- chairs, if minimally implied, sit in plausible relation to the seated person and table;
- shelf and partition have ordinary geometry;
- the walking path is unobstructed;
- floor/wall/partition perspective is coherent;
- the seated figure makes plausible contact with the desk area;
- the walking figure's position and motion make sense;
- light direction and shadows are spatially consistent.

Do not invent unusual furniture, impossible architecture, decorative structures, or ambiguous mechanical/structural details.

## Absolute exclusions

No readable computer displays.

Screens, if unavoidable as part of a workstation, must be dark or turned away and visually insignificant.

No:

- readable papers;
- readable books/spines;
- signs;
- labels;
- logos;
- branding;
- wall art;
- posters;
- captions;
- typography;
- UI;
- badges;
- decorative text;
- topic symbolism;
- publication layout inside the image;
- prior Portfilo wording.

## Rendering boundary

The image may use photographic spatial realism, but it must remain synthetic editorial artwork rather than documentary evidence of a real Portfilo office.

Target characteristics:

- believable compact-office perspective;
- observational rather than staged feeling;
- art-directed motion blur;
- dark monochrome tonality;
- visible editorial grain;
- no recognizable organization or real workplace.

## Success criteria

The candidate passes only if:

- the setting clearly reads as a small office/studio;
- exactly two people appear in the approved positions/roles;
- both people remain anonymous;
- furniture and circulation make immediate physical sense;
- no extra scene logic is invented;
- the left/central-upper overlay region stays dark and calm;
- the image is visibly grainy;
- no text/logo/UI contamination appears;
- the scene is aesthetically strong but remains subordinate to homepage HTML;
- the image does not explain or symbolize a topic.
