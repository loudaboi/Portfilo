## What improved

The candidate corrected several earlier execution failures:

- no generated typography;

Those improvements validate the newer generation controls around isolation, no-text enforcement, and lower scene complexity.

## Why it fails

The candidate fails the more important physical/logical test.

The curved wooden ribs and cross-members do not resolve into a clearly understandable or convincingly functional unfinished structure. The object creates a strong boat-hull association while its joints and overall geometry do not establish a coherent boat frame or another obvious construction system.

The visual therefore reads as invented generated geometry rather than a plausible object being built.

This is a material editorial-quality failure, not a minor aesthetic preference.

## Candidate status

The candidate may be retained only as process evidence showing that reducing scene complexity does not automatically solve **subject-level geometric complexity**.

## Lesson

Low scene complexity is insufficient when the dominant object itself requires the generator to invent complicated physical structure.

For future generation:

- prefer easily understandable spatial or material relationships;
- do not ask a generator to invent complicated joinery/structural systems unless the design is externally specified;
- reject an aesthetically strong object if its physical logic is unclear.
