## Why v3 exists

V2 improved scale and physical plausibility but still became too staged, literal, detailed, and descriptive of office work.

The homepage already supplies the actual information through HTML. The background only needs to provide a dark, grainy editorial atmosphere.

V3 therefore removes most scene content instead of asking the generator to make a simpler version of a furnished office.

## Direction

`OBSERVED WORKSPACE — PARTIAL OFFICE RECESS`

Use only a partial small-office setting rather than a complete room.

The image should feel like an observed fragment of a workplace, not a designed office scene.

## Image job

Create one publication-level homepage background whose job is atmosphere and visual identity only.

The image does not explain the story, topic, company, or work being performed.

## Scene lock

The scene contains exactly:

- one broad near-black wall/foreground plane occupying most of the left and central frame;
- one simple rectangular opening or recess on the right revealing part of a compact office/workroom;
- one plain rectangular desk inside the right-side recess;
- one ordinary chair aligned with the desk;
- exactly one anonymous motion-blurred human figure crossing the right-side opening/passage.

No other meaningful objects are authorized.

## Composition lock

- square source image;
- slightly elevated viewpoint;
- approximately 60-70% of the image remains dark, calm wall/floor geometry;
- the office recess occupies only the rightmost 30-40%;
- the desk and chair remain entirely inside that recess;
- the blurred figure crosses the right-side passage/opening and does not enter the left overlay-safe field;
- the left half and central-upper region remain predominantly near-black and low-detail for Portfilo's real light-blue HTML.

The visual hierarchy must be:

1. dark spatial field;
2. small office recess;
3. anonymous blurred figure.

The person must not become the focal subject.

## Office-detail ceiling

The office recess contains only:

- one desk;
- one chair;
- plain wall/floor surfaces.

No shelf, second table, lamp as a visible object, computer, monitor, papers, books, folders, stationery, plants, art, cabinet, couch, machinery, cables, decorative objects, or additional furniture.

The desk surface should be empty.

## Human-presence lock

Exactly one person:

- walking naturally across the right-side passage/opening;
- anonymous;
- visibly motion-blurred;
- dark clothing without marks;
- no readable face;
- no expressive gesture;
- no interaction with the desk;
- no duplicated limbs or ghost person.

No seated person and no additional silhouettes/reflections.

## Lighting and tone

- black and white / grayscale only;
- predominantly dark overall;
- one soft localized off-frame working-light zone inside the right-side office recess;
- the light source itself is not visible;
- enough illumination to reveal the desk/chair and basic office geometry;
- left and central field stays substantially darker;
- restrained highlights only;
- no theatrical spotlight, glowing effect, glossy commercial light, or cinematic spectacle.

## Grain

Visible grain is mandatory.

Target:

`MEDIUM-VISIBLE EDITORIAL GRAIN`

Grain should be clearly visible across dark and midtone wall/floor areas while preserving coherent edges and HTML-overlay readability.

Avoid clean digital smoothness, plastic texture, and aggressive denoising.

## Physical and logical plausibility

The setting must make immediate ordinary real-world sense.

Required checks:

- wall, opening/recess, floor and desk perspective align;
- desk stands normally and does not intersect walls;
- chair sits naturally at the desk;
- the walking passage is unobstructed;
- human scale matches desk, chair, opening and room;
- the blurred person retains plausible anatomy and stride;
- light direction and shadows are consistent with the off-frame right-side light zone;
- no impossible corners, floating surfaces, warped furniture, duplicate geometry or unexplained architectural structures.

A visually attractive candidate fails if the spatial logic is awkward or ambiguous.

## Absolute exclusions

No readable text anywhere.

No:

- signs;
- labels;
- logos;
- branding;
- slogans;
- captions;
- numbers;
- documents;
- books;
- screens;
- UI;
- wall art;
- posters;
- badges;
- arrows;
- borders;
- publication layout;
- topic symbolism;
- prior Portfilo wording;
- extra people;
- extra furniture or props.

## Rendering boundary

Use photographic spatial realism with clearly art-directed synthetic editorial treatment.

The image should feel observational and plausible, not like a documented real Portfilo office and not like a staged commercial office photograph.

Do not copy the composition of any supplied reference image.

## Success gate

Reject unless all are true:

- one partial compact-office recess only;
- exactly one desk;
- exactly one chair;
- exactly one blurred anonymous person;
- no other meaningful objects;
- 60-70% dark calm field survives for HTML;
- office recess stays on the right;
- physical/spatial logic is immediately coherent;
- medium-visible grain is clearly present;
- no readable text, logo, screen, book, paper, wall art or UI;
- person remains secondary;
- scene feels restrained and atmospheric rather than staged or descriptive.
