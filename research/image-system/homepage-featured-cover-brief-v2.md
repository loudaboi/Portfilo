## V2 revision goal

V1 established that the topic system can differentiate Building and AI while preserving one visual language, but all four candidates were too photographic and some scenes carried more detail than the homepage surface needs.

V2 changes only the rendering/detail discipline while preserving the strongest subject/composition logic.

## Complexity ceiling

Default for each homepage topic cover:

- **one environment**;
- **one dominant material/process anchor**;
- **one core visual relationship or action**;
- **zero or one secondary anonymous person**;
- **no additional semantic object unless it is necessary to understand the approved topic job**.

For the v2 Building + AI test, prefer **no person** unless the scene becomes unreadable without one.

## Detail budget

Additional detail must earn its place.

Do not add objects to make the scene feel richer, more realistic, more cinematic, more technical, or more complete.

Background information should primarily provide tonal context and scale, not introduce new narrative meaning.

Reject compositions that require a viewer to reconcile many tools, cables, machines, props, people, or simultaneous actions.

## Rendering guardrail

Required medium:

`REALISTIC EDITORIAL ILLUSTRATION / CLEARLY NON-PHOTOGRAPHIC`

Preserve:

- believable perspective;
- believable basic materials;
- believable structural relationships;
- directional light;
- grayscale;
- strong geometry;
- restrained texture/grain;
- serious editorial atmosphere.

Change from v1:

- simplify edges and surface detail;
- use larger graphic tonal shapes;
- use restrained print-like or drawn texture so the result reads as illustration;
- avoid camera-like depth cues whose main effect is to simulate photography.

Do not ask for:

- photorealism;
- real-photograph treatment;
- professional-photography treatment;
- camera/lens specifications;
- shallow depth of field;
- bokeh;
- cinematic camera effects;
- hyper-detailed material microtexture.

The asset may be realistic. It must not invite a reasonable reader to assume it is a photograph of a real workplace.

## Composition rule

The compact homepage cover remains the target.

Use a square, crop-flexible source for the pilot with:

- the dominant anchor near the crop-safe center or center-right;
- broad low-detail dark negative space through the left/central region for the real HTML topic label;
- no important meaning on the extreme edges;
- a strong silhouette and major light/dark relationship that survives reduction.

The image does not need to identify the topic without the HTML label.

## Building v2 subject lock

Preserve the useful logic from Building 01 without copying its rendering style:

- generic making/assembly environment;
- one unfinished curved wooden structural assembly as the dominant anchor;
- fitted/joined construction visibly in progress;
- no construction-site category shorthand;
- no additional narrative action required.

Building 01 is a **composition/subject lesson only**, not a style reference and not an approved final asset.

## AI v2 subject lock

Preserve the useful logic from AI 01 without copying its rendering style:

- generic machine-assisted work cell;
- one inspection/processing apparatus as the dominant anchor;
- short conveyor or process surface;
- a small number of repeated neutral units;
- one clear inspection/processing relationship;
- no humanoid or science-fiction AI symbolism.

AI 01 is a **composition/subject lesson only**, not a style reference and not an approved final asset.

## V2 test strategy

Generate only **one Building candidate and one AI candidate first** after separate authorization.

Reason:

V2 is testing one controlled change: whether simpler prompts, lower detail, and clearly illustrative rendering solve the v1 failure while preserving the approved topic logic.

If the treatment still fails, revise before spending another generation pass.

If both pass the treatment gate, decide whether a second candidate per topic is needed for composition choice.

## V2 success criteria

The pair passes only if:

- both are immediately readable as editorial illustration rather than documentary photography;
- each has one dominant anchor and no unnecessary semantic clutter;
- basic object relationships make physical sense;
- Building and AI remain clearly different topic worlds;
- both still belong to one Portfilo visual language;
- negative space supports the existing HTML label;
- the major composition survives compact display and responsive cropping;
- no text, logo, UI, brand, or identifiable real workplace appears.
