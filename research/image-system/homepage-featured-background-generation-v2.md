## Exact generator instruction v2

Create one **square monochrome editorial background image** of a **small, compact contemporary office or creative studio**, viewed from a **slightly elevated corner angle** so the viewer can understand the room layout at a glance.

### Room and furniture

The room contains exactly:

- **two simple rectangular worktables**;
- **one low shelving unit**;
- **one plain structural partition**;
- **clear walking space** between the furniture.

Place the first worktable in the deeper right side of the room. Place the second worktable farther forward on the right, separated from the first by a clear passage. Place the low shelving unit against the far-right or back-right wall so it does not block circulation. Use one plain partition to define the deeper work area without enclosing the room or creating unusual architecture.

Do not add additional desks, cabinets, couches, decorative furniture, plants, lamps as objects, machinery, architectural features, stairs, railings, mezzanines, unusual ceiling structures, or extra meaningful props.

Every table, shelf, partition, floor edge, wall edge, and walking path must use ordinary, mechanically simple geometry and make immediate physical sense.

### Human figures

Include **exactly two anonymous people** and no others.

Person 1:

- seated at the deeper-right worktable;
- body shown mainly as a dark, indistinct silhouette;
- normal seated posture facing the work surface;
- no recognizable face;
- no expressive gesture;
- visually secondary to the room.

Person 2:

- walking through the middle-right passage between the work areas;
- visibly motion-blurred from natural walking movement;
- no recognizable face;
- no expressive gesture;
- visually secondary to the room.

Do not add a third figure, reflection-person, portrait, face-like wall image, or human-shaped decoration.

### Composition and overlay zone

Keep the **entire left half** and the **central-upper region** predominantly near-black, calm, and low-detail so Portfilo's real light-blue HTML can sit above the image.

Concentrate furniture detail, localized illumination, and both human figures on the **right half**.

The image must feel compact and intimate rather than monumental, cavernous, institutional, or museum-like.

The room architecture must remain simple enough that floor, walls, partition, furniture, and walking path are understandable immediately.

### Lighting and tone

Use **black and white / grayscale only**.

Overall exposure must remain dark.

Use **one restrained localized practical working-light zone on the right side**, illuminating portions of the worktables and nearby floor without brightening the whole room.

Keep the left half and central-upper overlay area substantially darker than the right-side work zone.

Use deep blacks and restrained highlights. Avoid dramatic spotlighting, theatrical beams, glowing effects, glossy advertising light, or cinematic spectacle.

### Grain and rendering

Apply **clearly visible medium editorial grain** across the image, especially visible in dark and midtone wall/floor areas.

The grain must be noticeable but must not obscure furniture geometry, the walking path, human silhouettes, or the future HTML overlay.

Avoid clean digital smoothness, plastic surfaces, excessive denoising, and glossy rendering.

Use an observational synthetic-editorial treatment with believable spatial realism. The scene must not imply that it is a documentary photograph of a real Portfilo office.

### Physical and logical plausibility

The scene must obey ordinary real-world spatial logic:

- all four table legs/supports must resolve plausibly where visible;
- tables must not intersect walls, partition, shelf, or people;
- the seated figure must align naturally with the deeper-right worktable;
- any minimally visible chair must be structurally ordinary and aligned with the seated figure;
- the walking person must have an unobstructed passage;
- shelf and partition must sit squarely on the floor and connect to the room normally;
- wall/floor perspective must be coherent;
- scale between people, tables, shelf, partition, and room must be consistent;
- motion blur may soften the walking figure but must not create extra limbs or duplicate body parts;
- light direction and cast shadows must be consistent with the right-side working-light zone.

Do not invent hidden engineering, unusual joinery, impossible supports, floating objects, warped furniture, impossible intersections, or decorative structures.

### Absolute exclusions

No readable text anywhere.

No:

- signs;
- labels;
- logos;
- branding;
- slogans;
- captions;
- numbers;
- readable documents;
- readable books or book spines;
- wall art;
- posters;
- badges;
- publication design;
- UI;
- arrows;
- borders;
- prior Portfilo wording;
- topic symbolism.

Computer displays, if minimally necessary to make the deeper-right desk read as a workstation, must be dark, turned away, unlit, unreadable, and visually insignificant. Prefer no visible screen at all.

Do not add anything merely to make the room feel richer, busier, more realistic, or more cinematic.
